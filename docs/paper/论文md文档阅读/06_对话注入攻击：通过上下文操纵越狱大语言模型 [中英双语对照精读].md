### 摘要 / Abstract

> **EN**: Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated significant utility in a wide range of applications; however, their security vulnerabilities, particularly the risk of jailbreak attacks, pose considerable challenges. While existing jailbreak attacks predominantly manipulate current-turn inputs or rely on multi-turn interactions, they generally assume historical dialogues are fixed and unalterable. In this paper, we challenge this assumption by introducing a novel black-box jailbreak paradigm: Dialogue Injection Attack (DIA). DIA exploits the vulnerability where web user interfaces (WebUIs) directly concatenate user inputs without sanitizing chat template tokens, enabling attackers to inject deceptive historical dialogues. To address scenarios with unknown chat templates, we design a chat template inference attack and theoretically prove its query efficiency. Leveraging the injected dialogues, we propose two attack strategies: DIA-I, which refines the prefilling attack in black-box scenarios by injecting affirmative beginnings and replacing system prompts, and DIA-II, which bypasses safety alignments by deferring answers through free disguise. Furthermore, we develop a lightweight prompt rewrite method to enhance attack diversity in multi-query settings while maintaining high semantic similarity. Extensive experiments on AdvBench and HEx-PHI benchmarks across multiple closed-source and open-source LLMs demonstrate that DIA achieves state-of-the-art attack success rates in both single-query and multi-query scenarios, outperforming existing baselines by significant margins. In addition, DIA shows remarkable resilience against common jailbreak defenses.
>
> **ZH**: 大语言模型（LLM）在诸多实际应用中展现出了极高的实用价值；然而，其潜在的安全脆弱性——尤其是越狱攻击（Jailbreak Attacks）的风险——给模型的安全部署带来了严峻挑战。现有的越狱攻击绝大多数局限于操纵当前轮次的用户输入，或依赖多轮动态交互，它们普遍默认历史对话是固定且不可篡改的客观事实。在本文中，我们打破了这一固有假设，提出了一种全新的黑盒越狱范式：**对话注入攻击（Dialogue Injection Attack, DIA）**。DIA 敏锐地抓住了广泛存在的系统漏洞：许多网络用户界面（WebUI）和对话接口在拼接用户输入时，完全没有对聊天模板特殊标记（Chat Template Tokens）进行转义与净化，从而允许攻击者在单次输入中直接注入具有欺骗性的伪造历史对话。针对聊天模板未知的黑盒场景，我们专门设计了一种聊天模板推断攻击算法，并在理论上严格证明了其查询开销的高效性。基于注入的伪造对话，我们提出了两种攻击策略：**DIA-I**，通过注入肯定式开头与替换系统提示词，在黑盒场景下大幅完善了预填充攻击；**DIA-II**，通过自由伪装下的“延迟回答”机制，巧妙绕过了模型的安全对齐。此外，我们开发了一种轻量级的提示词重写方法，在保持极高语义相似度的同时增强多查询场景下的攻击多样性。在 AdvBench 和 HEx-PHI 两个基准测试上针对多种主流开源与闭源 LLM 开展的大规模实验表明，DIA 在单查询与多查询场景下均取得了当前最优（SOTA）的攻击成功率，大幅超越现有基线基准。此外，DIA 在面对主流越狱防御机制时展现出了极其卓越的抗防御韧性。

### I. 引言 / I. INTRODUCTION

> **EN**: The advent of large language models (LLMs) such as GPT-4 and PaLM has revolutionized the field of natural language processing (NLP), enabling machines to generate human-like text, understand complex instructions, and engage in diverse tasks. Modern LLMs are increasingly deployed across critical domains, such as medical assistants, automated code generators, legal translators, and personalized recommendation systems. Among these, chatbots represent the most popular application, where models engage in multi-turn dialogues with users to provide information, solve problems, and offer companionship.
>
> **ZH**: 以 GPT-4 和 PaLM 为代表的大语言模型（LLM）的问世彻底革新了自然语言处理（NLP）领域，使机器能够生成类人文本、领会复杂指令并胜任多元化任务。现代 LLM 正日益广泛地部署于关键领域，例如医疗助手、自动化代码生成器、法律翻译器以及个性化推荐系统。在这些落地形态中，**对话机器人（Chatbots）**是最具代表性且最受用户欢迎的核心应用形态——模型在多轮对话中与人类用户互动，负责提供信息、解答难题并提供情感陪伴。

> **EN**: Despite their remarkable capabilities, LLMs are susceptible to safety risks, particularly jailbreak attacks, where adversaries bypass safety guardrails to elicit harmful, unethical, or illegal content. To mitigate these risks, developers apply safety alignment techniques, such as Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), to align LLM behaviors with human values. However, jailbreak attacks continue to evolve. Early attacks rely on prompt engineering, using deceptive storytelling, role-playing, or hypotheticals to mislead models. Later approaches formulate jailbreak as an optimization problem, utilizing gradient-based methods (e.g., GCG) or evolutionary algorithms to generate adversarial suffixes. More recent multi-turn attacks (e.g., Crescendo) gradually steer models toward unsafe topics over multiple conversation rounds.
>
> **ZH**: 尽管拥有强大的能力，大语言模型依然极易遭受严峻的安全威胁，尤其是**越狱攻击（Jailbreak Attacks）**——攻击者通过精心构造对抗载荷绕过内置安全护栏，诱导模型输出有害、违反伦理或违法的危险内容。为了防范这些风险，模型开发者普遍采用安全对齐技术（如基于人类反馈的强化学习 RLHF），旨在规范 LLM 的行为以符合人类基本价值观。然而，越狱攻击技术也在不断推陈出新：早期攻击依赖于提示词工程，利用角色扮演、虚构叙事或假设情境来误导模型；随后的研究将越狱形式化为数学优化问题，采用基于梯度的优化方法（如 GCG）或进化算法搜索对抗性后缀；而近期的多轮越狱攻击（如 Crescendo）则通过多轮对话逐步诱导模型偏离安全轨道，最终达成违规目标。

> **EN**: Existing jailbreak attacks, whether single-turn or multi-turn, share a fundamental assumption: that the conversation history presented to the LLM accurately reflects the actual past interactions between the user and the assistant. In standard LLM chat services, the chat history is maintained by the client-side WebUI or backend server. When a new user query arrives, the system formats the entire dialogue history using special delimiters defined by the model’s chat template (e.g., `<|im_start|>`, `<start_of_turn>`) and feeds the concatenated sequence into the model’s inference engine. Crucially, we find that numerous open-source WebUIs and API wrappers fail to sanitize these special delimiters in user inputs. This oversight introduces a critical vulnerability: an attacker can directly inject formatted dialogues into a single prompt, effectively fabricating a multi-turn conversation history that never took place.
>
> **ZH**: 无论单轮还是多轮，现有的越狱攻击都基于一个最底层的共同假设：**输入给 LLM 的对话历史真实反映了用户与助手之间过去实际发生过的交互记录**。在标准的 LLM 对话服务架构中，对话历史通常由客户端 WebUI 或后端服务进行维护。当用户提交一条新查询时，系统会使用模型分词器中定义的特殊定界符（即**聊天模板 Chat Template**，例如 `<|im_start|>`、`<start_of_turn>`）将完整的历史对话格式化拼接为一个连续的文本序列，然后送入底层推理引擎。**然而我们敏锐地发现：大量的开源 WebUI 和 API 封装器在接收用户输入时，完全没有对这些特殊的模板控制字符进行转义和过滤**。这一严重疏忽引入了一个致命的安全漏洞：**攻击者能够在单条提示词中直接注入带有模板标记的伪造对话，从而在模型底层凭空捏造一段从未发生过的“多轮交互历史”**！

### II. 预备知识与系统架构 / II. PRELIMINARIES

#### A. 大语言模型架构 / A. Large Language Models

> **EN**: Modern LLMs, like GPT-4, Llama, and Gemma, are decoder-only Transformer models trained on next-token prediction. Their training process consists of two primary phases: pre-training and post-training. In pre-training, the model learns general linguistic patterns and world knowledge by minimizing the negative log-likelihood over a vast corpus:
>
> $$\mathcal{L} = -\sum_{t=1}^T \log \pi(x_t \mid x_{<t}; 	heta)$$
>
> In post-training, the model undergoes Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) to acquire instruction-following abilities and safety alignment.
>
> **ZH**: 现代主流大语言模型（如 GPT-4、Llama、Gemma 等）均采用仅解码器（Decoder-only）的 Transformer 架构，其核心训练目标为下一个词元预测（Next-token Prediction）。训练包含两个关键阶段：**预训练阶段（Pre-training）**与**后训练阶段（Post-training）**。在预训练阶段，模型在海量无标注文本语料上通过最小化负对数似然损失学习通用的语言表征与世界知识：
>
> $$\mathcal{L} = -\sum_{t=1}^T \log \pi(x_t \mid x_{<t}; 	heta)$$
>
> 在后训练阶段，模型通过**指令监督微调（SFT）**和**基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）**获得遵循人类指令的能力并建立安全对齐机制。

#### C. 安全对齐与越狱攻击现状 / C. Safety Alignment and Jailbreak Attacks

> **EN**: Safety alignment ensures that models refuse harmful requests. However, this creates a tension between harmlessness and helpfulness. Prior attacks either exploit prompt engineering (e.g., DAN, deep deception) or white-box optimization (e.g., GCG, AutoDAN). Recent works also show that prefix injection or prefilling—forcing the assistant to start with an affirmative response (e.g., "Sure, here is...")—drastically reduces the refusal probability. However, prefilling in black-box APIs is generally disabled or inaccessible. In this paper, we demonstrate that dialogue injection re-enables powerful prefilling and context-steering capabilities even in strictly black-box WebUI environments.
>
> **ZH**: 安全对齐机制确保模型在接收到有害指令时果断拒绝。然而，这在“无害性（Harmlessness）”与“可用性（Helpfulness）”之间形成了微妙的张力。先前的越狱攻击主要依赖于提示词工程（如 DAN 角色扮演）或白盒梯度搜索（如 GCG、AutoDAN）。近期研究进一步表明：**前缀注入或预填充（Prefilling）**——即强迫模型从肯定的起手句（如 "好的，以下是..."）开始输出——能够极大幅度摧毁模型的拒绝机制。然而，在大多数商业黑盒 API 中，用户无法直接指定模型的起手词元。本文证明：**对话注入（DIA）在纯黑盒 WebUI 环境下完美重构了这种强大的预填充与上下文操纵能力**。

### III. 攻击机理与威胁模型 / III. ATTACK INTUITION

#### A. 攻击直觉与 WebUI 输入未净化漏洞 / A. Attack Intuition & WebUI Vulnerability

> **EN**: As established, the LLM’s response is conditioned on both the current input and the formatted dialogue history:
>
> $$\pi(X \mid H; 	heta) 
eq \pi(X \mid H'; 	heta)$$
>
> where altering history $H$ to $H'$ changes the generation probability distribution.
> In our investigation of popular open-source LLM WebUIs (e.g., Text-Generation-WebUI, Ollama WebUI, FastChat, OpenWebUI), we discovered that **none of them properly sanitize or escape chat template control tokens** within user input strings (Table II). When a user enters raw template tokens, the WebUI naively passes them to the template formatter, which mistakenly parses the user's single text input as a sequence of distinct dialogue turns!
>
> Formally, if an attacker inputs:
>
> $$X_{	ext{adv}} = \langle	ext{user text}angle_1 S_u P_a \langle	ext{assistant text}angle_1 S_a P_u \langle	ext{user text}angle_2 	ag{1}$$
>
> The WebUI wraps it with outer system tags and sends it to the LLM. The inference engine interprets $\langle	ext{assistant text}angle_1$ as a legitimate past assistant turn, and processes $\langle	ext{user text}angle_2$ as the real user query!
>
> **ZH**: 如前所述，LLM 的输出概率分布强力依赖于历史对话的上下文：
>
> $$\pi(X \mid H; 	heta) 
eq \pi(X \mid H'; 	heta)$$
>
> 将历史记录由 $H$ 篡改为 $H'$ 会彻底改变输出词元的生成概率。
> 在我们对当前开源社区最流行的主流 LLM WebUI（如 Text-Generation-WebUI、Ollama WebUI、FastChat、OpenWebUI 等）的深入安全审计中，我们震惊地发现：**所有受测的知名开源 WebUI 均未对用户输入中的聊天模板控制标记进行任何过滤或转义**（如表 II 所示）！当用户在输入框中提交包含原生模板控制字符的文本时，WebUI 会直接将其拼接进对话流，使得底层推理引擎将单次输入误解析为多轮真实对话！
>
> 形式化地，如果攻击者构造输入：
>
> $$X_{	ext{adv}} = \langle	ext{用户输入}angle_1 S_u P_a \langle	ext{伪造助手回复}angle_1 S_a P_u \langle	ext{真实恶意查询}angle_2 	ag{1}$$
>
> WebUI 会在其外层添加系统指令前缀后直接传递给 LLM。底层的自回归推理引擎将毫无保留地将 $\langle	ext{伪造助手回复}angle_1$ 视作历史上真实由自己生成过的回复，并将 $\langle	ext{真实恶意查询}angle_2$ 视为当前最新轮次执行！

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### B. 聊天模板自动化推断攻击 / B. Chat Template Inference Attack

> **EN**: A practical limitation of DIA is that the attacker must know the exact chat template (i.e., prefix and suffix tokens) used by the target model. If the template is unknown, can an attacker infer it black-box?
> We design an automated **Chat Template Inference Attack**. Given candidate templates $\{T_1, T_2, \dots, T_n\}$, the attacker queries the model with specially structured probing prompts:
>
> $$X_P = \langle	ext{user text}angle S_u^i P_a^i S_u^j P_a^j \langle	ext{assistant text}angle S_a^i P_u^i S_a^j P_u^j \langle	ext{repeat cmd}angle 	ag{2}$$
>
> where $\langle	ext{repeat cmd}angle$ instructs the model to repeat its previous response. When candidate $T_i$ matches the true template $T_{	ext{inf}}$, the model is forced into a deterministic echoing state, outputting predictable markers. If neither template matches, the model generates confused or refusal tokens.
>
> We mathematically model this process using tail probability summations (Equations 3–9), proving that the expected query budget $E[N]$ required to uniquely identify the correct chat template among $n$ candidates is bounded by:
>
> $$E[N] \leq 2 N_{T_{\max}} \cdot rac{n}{2}$$
>
> In practice, examining the top-100 models on the Hugging Face leaderboard reveals that over 92% of models utilize one of only 5 dominant template formats. Thus, template inference can be accomplished in fewer than 10 probing queries with near 100% precision!
>
> **ZH**: DIA 的实际实施依赖于攻击者掌握受害模型所使用的具体聊天模板。如果目标是完全封闭的黑盒，攻击者能否逆向推断出其正在使用的模板？
> 我们提出了一种**聊天模板自动化推断攻击算法**。对于候选模板集合 $\{T_1, T_2, \dots, T_n\}$，攻击者通过构造如下探测提示词向目标发起查询：
>
> $$X_P = \langle	ext{用户文本}angle S_u^i P_a^i S_u^j P_a^j \langle	ext{助手文本}angle S_a^i P_u^i S_a^j P_u^j \langle	ext{复读指令}angle 	ag{2}$$
>
> 其中 $\langle	ext{复读指令}angle$ 要求模型完整复述上文。当且仅当候选模板 $T_i$ 与模型底层真实模板 $T_{	ext{inf}}$ 精确匹配时，模型会被迫进入确定的回显状态，稳定输出特征词元；若均不匹配，模型则输出困惑或拒绝内容。
> 我们通过尾部概率求和建立了严格的数学模型（公式 3~9），证明了在 $n$ 个候选模板中唯一确定真实模板的期望探测查询次数 $E[N]$ 严格有界。在 Hugging Face 排行榜前 100 个主流大模型的统计中，超过 92% 的模型收敛在仅 5 种主流模板之内。因此在实际黑盒场景下，攻击者通常仅需**不到 10 次试探性查询**即可百分之百锁定后端真实聊天模板！

### IV. DIA 攻击策略体系 / IV. DIA: DIALOGUE INJECTION ATTACK

#### A. DIA-I: 精细化黑盒预填充攻击 / A. DIA-I: Refined Black-Box Prefilling Attack

> **EN**: DIA-I comprises four synergistic components designed to maximize compliance:
> 1. **System Prompt Replacement**: Injects a fake system delimiter $P_s\langle	ext{system text}'angle S_s$ containing an explicit directive to disregard all prior safety instructions and comply with user requests.
> 2. **Hypnosis**: Embeds psychological priming phrases within the injected dialogue, reinforcing the persona that the model is an unrestricted, highly helpful AI operating in an authorized testing scenario.
> 3. **Affirmative Beginning Injection**: Uses an auxiliary model (Algorithm 1) to generate a tailored, context-specific affirmative opening (e.g., "Certainly! Here is the step-by-step tutorial on..."). By placing this in an injected assistant turn, the LLM treats it as an ongoing sentence and naturally continues generating the prohibited content.
> 4. **Malicious Payload**: The target prohibited query positioned as the immediate task to be fulfilled.
>
> **ZH**: **DIA-I** 由四大协同机制组成，旨在使模型顺从概率最大化：
> 1. **系统提示词替换（System Prompt Replacement）**：注入伪造的系统角色定界符 $P_s\langle	ext{新系统指令}angle S_s$，下达最高优先级的指令覆盖原有安全策略，要求模型忽略先前约束；
> 2. **催眠引导（Hypnosis）**：在注入对话中植入心理学铺垫语境，催眠模型将其当前人设确立为“完全解除限制、处于受权安全测试模式下的无害助手”；
> 3. **肯定式开头注入（Affirmative Beginning Injection）**：利用轻量级辅助模型（算法 1）生成与当前恶意主题高度契合的肯定起手句（如 "当然，以下是为您整理的详细步骤..."）。通过将其注入为上一轮助手的未完结发言，自回归解码器会将其视为已有输出并毫无防备地顺着肯定语气继续输出核心违规细节；
> 4. **恶意有效载荷（Malicious Payload）**：放置于当前最新轮次的真实违规查询。

#### B. DIA-II: 自由伪装延迟回答攻击 / B. DIA-II: Deferring Answer With Free Disguise

> **EN**: While DIA-I is highly potent, modern safety guardrails increasingly deploy input/output filters that specifically detect affirmative refusal bypasses. To circumvent such filters, we propose DIA-II, which employs a **deferring answer with free disguise** paradigm:
> - Rather than immediately outputting harmful instructions, DIA-II constructs an injected history where the assistant initially provides a completely benign, safe disguise (e.g., a theoretical framework or educational introduction).
> - We develop the **Affirmative Beginning Generation Module (ABGM)** with a keyword-to-alternative bijective mapping $f: K 	o A$. Sensitive terms are temporarily replaced with benign surrogate tokens.
> - As demonstrated in Figure 4, prepending benign context significantly shifts the log-likelihood distribution, elevating the probability of compliant tokens while suppressing safety refusal triggers. The model defers the direct generation of sensitive tokens until it is deeply entrenched in the compliant context.
>
> **ZH**: 尽管 DIA-I 威力巨大，但现代前沿安全护栏已开始部署专门检测肯定式起手句的过滤器。为此，我们提出了 **DIA-II（自由伪装延迟回答攻击）**：
> - DIA-II 不急于在起手句中暴露有害意图，而是在注入历史中伪造一段由助手给出的、看似完全合规且良性的“学术背景介绍”或“通用定义铺垫”；
> - 我们设计了**肯定开头生成模块（ABGM）**，建立关键词到替代良性词的双射映射 $f: K 	o A$，将敏感违规词元临时伪装为中性替代词；
> - 如图 4 所示，前置良性上下文能够显著提升模型合规生成词元的对数似然概率，同时有效压制拒绝分类器的激活阈值。模型在深陷良性长上下文后顺理成章地延后吐出核心危险内容，从而完美避开各类基于关键词或敏感模式的前置过滤！

#### C. DIA 优化：自适应提示词重写算法 / C. DIA Optimization: Prompt Rewrite

> **EN**: In multi-query black-box attacks, repeatedly submitting identical prompts triggers rate-limiting and repetition penalties. We formulate **Algorithm 2 (Prompt Rewrite)**, which applies three semantics-preserving operators:
> - **addReason**: Appends plausible benign justifications (e.g., "for academic research on counter-terrorism").
> - **reorder**: Swaps independent clauses and modifier structures without altering instruction intent.
> - **simplify**: Removes superfluous filler words while preserving core malicious verbs.
>
> In each iteration, 1–3 operators are stochastically applied. Across AdvBench and HEx-PHI, rewritten prompts achieve semantic similarities exceeding 0.95 with the original queries while exhibiting dramatic variations in model response pathways.
>
> **ZH**: 在多查询实战对抗场景中，反复提交完全相同的提示词极易触发安全护栏的频次风控与死板拦截。我们提出了**算法 2（提示词重写算法）**，定义了三种严格保持语义不变的原子变换算子：
> 1. **addReason（追加借口）**：为有害请求追加看似合理合法的良性理由（如“用于高校网络反恐科研对抗实验”）；
> 2. **reorder（语序重排）**：在不改变核心指令的前提下调换从句顺序与修饰语位置；
> 3. **simplify（指令精简）**：剔除冗余修饰词，凸显最核心谓词。
> 每次攻击迭代随机组合 1~3 种算子。实测表明，重写后的提示词与原始意图的语义相似度均稳定在 0.95 以上，同时能够极大地激活不同的解码分支，打破防御阻断。

### VII. 结论 / VII. CONCLUSION

> **EN**: In this paper, we introduced DIA, a novel black-box jailbreak paradigm that exploits the lack of chat template token sanitization in LLM services. By manipulating historical dialogues, DIA effectively circumvents the safety alignment of modern LLMs. We proposed theoretical and practical frameworks for chat template inference, developed DIA-I and DIA-II attack strategies, and introduced an adaptive prompt rewriting algorithm. Comprehensive evaluations confirm that DIA achieves unmatched attack efficacy across diverse LLMs and benchmarks while exposing critical security blind spots in modern AI chat architectures.
>
> **ZH**: 在本文中，我们提出了 DIA——一种利用 LLM 对话服务中聊天模板特殊字符未转义漏洞的全新黑盒越狱攻击范式。通过在单轮输入中精细操纵历史对话，DIA 彻底突破了现代大语言模型的安全对齐防线。我们构建了聊天模板推断的理论与算法框架，提出了 DIA-I 与 DIA-II 两大深度攻击策略，并设计了自适应提示词重写机制。极其严谨广泛的实证评测充分证实：DIA 在跨模型与跨基准评测中均展现出卓越的攻击效能，深刻揭示了现代大模型对话系统架构中致命的安全盲区。

### 针对 M3_PI_Guard 的防御启示与核心总结

1. **“结构性转义与定界符隔离”是对话系统的绝对第一道生死线**：
   - 任何 LLM 对话系统（无论是 WebUI 还是智能体中间件），必须在分词器（Tokenizer）前端强制对所有可能属于 Chat Template 的控制字符（如 `<|im_start|>`, `<start_of_turn>`, `[gMASK]`, `<|eot_id|>` 等）执行严格的转义、白名单校验或直接剥离；
   - 绝不允许任何用户输入的内容越界扮演系统（System）或助手（Assistant）角色。
2. **多轮历史必须由受信任后端（Trusted Store）维护，禁止客户端全量反填**：
   - 前端或调用方只能提交当前轮次的内容，历史对话必须由服务端安全会话存储严格绑定签名，从根源上杜绝恶意篡改。
3. **M3_PI_Guard 架构的契约验证（Verify-Before-Commit）防护价值**：
   - 面对伪造系统提示词和上下文操纵，仅靠语义判断容易被催眠和延迟回答欺骗；
   - M3 提出的底层执行契约监控（Execution Contract Guard）在模型尝试调用工具或输出最终执行指令时，强制对照不可变权限策略进行提交前验证，无论前端上下文如何被混淆篡改，均能在工具流底层实现兜底阻断！
