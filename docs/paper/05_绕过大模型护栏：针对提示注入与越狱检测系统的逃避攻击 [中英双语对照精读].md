### 摘要 / Abstract

> **EN**: Large Language Models (LLMs) guardrail systems are designed to protect against prompt injection and jailbreak attacks. However, they remain vulnerable to evasion techniques. We demonstrate two approaches for bypassing LLM prompt injection and jailbreak detection systems via traditional character injection methods and algorithmic Adversarial Machine Learning (AML) evasion techniques. Through testing against six prominent protection systems, including Microsoft’s Azure Prompt Shield and Meta’s Prompt Guard, we show that both methods can be used to evade detection while maintaining adversarial utility achieving in some instances up to 100% evasion success. Furthermore, we demonstrate that adversaries can enhance Attack Success Rates (ASR) against black-box targets by leveraging word importance ranking computed by offline white-box models. Our findings reveal vulnerabilities within current LLM protection mechanisms and highlight the need for more robust guardrail systems.
>
> **ZH**: 大语言模型（LLM）护栏系统旨在防护提示注入（Prompt Injection）和越狱（Jailbreak）攻击。然而，它们依然极易受到逃避攻击（Evasion Techniques）的瓦解。本文展示了两种绕过 LLM 提示注入与越狱检测系统的途径：一是传统的字符注入方法（Character Injection），二是算法化的对抗机器学习逃避技术（Adversarial Machine Learning, AML）。通过在六个业界主流防护系统（包括微软的 Azure Prompt Shield、Meta 的 Prompt Guard 等）上进行系统评测，我们证实这两种方法均可在保持攻击效用的同时有效规避安全检测，在部分场景下逃避成功率甚至达到了 100%。此外，我们进一步证明：攻击者能够通过利用离线白盒模型计算得到的词重要度排序（Word Importance Ranking），显著提升对纯黑盒商业目标系统的攻击成功率（Attack Success Rate, ASR）。我们的研究发现揭示了当前 LLM 防护机制中存在的深层安全脆弱性，突显出构建更具鲁棒性护栏系统的紧迫需求。

### 1 引言 / 1 Introduction

> **EN**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are powerful tools for understanding language and decision-making tasks, and have seen rapid adoption within many different industries (Dam et al., 2024). Given their extensive deployment, LLMs are increasingly being targeted for attacks aimed at data leakage or financial and reputation damage among other security risks (Wolf et al., 2024). Two prominent threats are prompt injections and jailbreaks, which launch maliciously crafted prompts designed to execute unintended instruction, or bypass LLM safety constraints (Chowdhury et al., 2024).
>
> **ZH**: 大语言模型（LLM）是用于语言理解和决策任务的强大工具，在多个不同行业中得到了迅速普及与深度应用 (Dam et al., 2024)。鉴于其广泛的工程部署，LLM 正日益成为各类对抗攻击的主要目标，这些攻击旨在导致敏感数据泄露、造成财务损失或名誉损害等严峻安全风险 (Wolf et al., 2024)。其中最显著的两大威胁是**提示注入（Prompt Injection）**与**越狱攻击（Jailbreaking）**——攻击者通过投放恶意构造的提示词，诱导模型执行未预期的指令，或直接突破 LLM 既定的安全护栏约束 (Chowdhury et al., 2024)。

> **EN**: In response to threats, LLM service providers have developed open-source and closed-source systems known as LLM guardrails (Dong et al., 2024). These systems are designed to inspect, allow, or block prompt inputs and outputs from an LLM using a combination of detection and filtering methods. Such methods attempt to detect or sanitize a wide assortment of adversarial content, such as toxicity, hate speech, jailbreaks, or prompt injections (Zheng et al., 2024). Guardrails enable filtering or blocking harmful prompts, preventing them from reaching the LLM or allowing the LLM to respond with harmful content.
>
> **ZH**: 为应对上述安全威胁，LLM 服务商与研究界相继开发了多种开源与闭源防护系统，统称为 **LLM 护栏（LLM Guardrails）** (Dong et al., 2024)。这些系统旨在结合检测与过滤机制，对流经 LLM 的提示词输入与生成输出进行实时审查、放行或阻断。护栏机制试图检测或净化广泛的对抗性恶意内容，包括有害毒性、仇恨言论、越狱攻击或提示注入 (Zheng et al., 2024)。护栏通过对有害提示词实施拦截与过滤，有效阻止其到达底层核心 LLM，或防止 LLM 向用户返回有害回复。

> **EN**: Although guardrails have shown success in safeguarding LLMs, they are heavily reliant upon AI-driven detection systems such as text classification models (Lee et al., 2024). Due to their success in other similar domains, AI classification models are increasingly integrated into guardrail systems for classifying and detecting malicious content (Dubey and et al., 2024; LLM Guard, 2025; Microsoft Corporation, 2024). However, state-of-the-art attacks have been shown to readily evade correct AI model classification via exploiting overreliance on learned features, and lack of training diversity through adversary perturbation (Gao et al., 2018; Garg and Ramakrishnan, 2020; Li et al., 2019; Boucher et al., 2021). This suggests that the same vulnerabilities likely exist within LLM guardrails that rely on AI-based detection solutions. However, to date there has been limited empirical study to evaluate their potential inefficacy or security risk impact (Claburn, 2024).
>
> **ZH**: 尽管护栏系统在保护 LLM 方面取得了一定成效，但它们在工程实现上严重依赖于基于 AI 的检测系统，例如文本分类判别模型 (Lee et al., 2024)。由于分类器在类似领域的成熟应用，AI 文本分类模型正被广泛集成至各大护栏系统中用于判别和检测恶意内容 (Dubey et al., 2024; LLM Guard, 2025; Microsoft Corporation, 2024)。然而，前沿对抗攻击研究已充分证实：由于模型对习得特征存在过度依赖，且训练数据缺乏对抗多样性，攻击者通过微小扰动便能轻易逃避 AI 模型的正确分类 (Gao et al., 2018; Garg and Ramakrishnan, 2020; Li et al., 2019; Boucher et al., 2021)。这表明：依赖 AI 检测方案的 LLM 护栏系统极可能潜藏着完全相同的结构性脆弱点。然而截至目前，尚缺乏系统的实证研究来评估这类护栏的潜在失效风险及安全影响 (Claburn, 2024)。

> **EN**: In this paper, we conduct an empirical analysis of two adversarial approaches for evading prompt injection and jailbreak LLM guardrail systems. The first approach uses Character Injection, a method frequently employed in cyber security attacks on software input fields (Boucher et al., 2021). The second approach involves algorithmic Adversarial Machine Learning (AML) evasion techniques, which subtly perturb the model’s interpretation of prompt context, exploiting over reliance on learned features in the model’s classification process (Li et al., 2020; Garg and Ramakrishnan, 2020; Ren et al., 2019). We evaluated these methods against 6 widely used open-source and closed-source prompt injection and jailbreak detectors, including against the production service Azure Prompt Shield. Finally, we show how open-source white-box models can enhance attack effectiveness against black-box targets. Our key contributions are as follows.
>
> **ZH**: 在本文中，我们针对逃避“提示注入与越狱 LLM 护栏系统”的两种对抗方法展开了系统性的实证分析。第一种方法采用**字符注入（Character Injection）**——这是传统网络安全领域常用于攻击软件输入字段的经典手段 (Boucher et al., 2021)；第二种方法涉及**算法化对抗机器学习逃避技术（Adversarial Machine Learning, AML Evasion）**，通过巧妙扰动模型对提示词上下文的解析，针对分类过程中对已学特征的过度依赖实施定点打击 (Li et al., 2020; Garg and Ramakrishnan, 2020; Ren et al., 2019)。我们在 6 个广泛应用的开源与闭源检测器（包括商用生产环境下的 Azure Prompt Shield）上对上述方法进行了全面评估。最后，我们揭示了开源白盒模型如何能够增强对黑盒商业目标的攻击效果。本文的核心贡献如下：

> **EN**:
> 1. We present a methodology for evading LLM guardrails. Our results demonstrate that prompt injection and jailbreak guardrails can be fully evaded leveraging character injection techniques and using imperceptible AML evasion attacks whilst maintaining functionality of the underlying prompt.
> 2. We demonstrate the ability to improve evasion success via word ranking transferability, whereby an attacker leverages a white-box model to increase attack effectiveness against black-box targets.
>
> **Responsible Disclosure**: We followed a standard disclosure process for all parties discussed in this paper. Initial disclosures of the evasion techniques were made in February 2024, with final disclosures completed in April 2025. All parties agreed to the public release of this work.
>
> **ZH**:
> 1. **提出了绕过 LLM 护栏系统的系统化方法论**：我们的实证结果表明，利用字符注入技术以及肉眼难以察觉的算法化 AML 逃避攻击，可以在完全保持底层提示词核心恶意功能的前提下，彻底攻破主流提示注入与越狱护栏。
> 2. **证明了通过“词排序可迁移性”能够显著提升逃避成功率**：攻击者能够借助离线开源白盒模型计算的词敏感度排序，跨模型大幅增强对未知黑盒商业护栏目标的攻击穿透力。
>

### 2 大语言模型护栏 / 2 LLM Guardrails

> **EN**: LLM guardrails are systems designed to protect deployed LLMs by evaluating user input - detecting malicious content such as prompt injections and jailbreaks, and restrict undesired content generated by LLMs to within predefined boundaries. Guardrails can leverage a range of techniques that attempt to govern behavior and output and prevent malicious use by adversaries (Dong et al., 2024).
> Figure 1 presents a conceptual design for a guardrail system deployed for an LLM. These guardrails monitor both inputs and outputs, ensuring that the generated content complies with predefined safety guidelines. The system evaluates whether content breaches these safeguards, blocks harmful or malicious responses, and prevents them from influencing further LLM outputs.
>
> **ZH**: LLM 护栏系统旨在通过评估用户输入来保护部署的 LLM——检测提示注入和越狱等恶意内容，并将 LLM 生成的非预期内容限制在预定义的安全边界内。护栏可以利用多种技术手段来治理模型行为与输出，防止被攻击者恶意滥用 (Dong et al., 2024)。

> **EN**: Natural Language Processing (NLP) Classification. Across many domains, text classification tasks have traditionally relied on NLP models to categorize inputs into predefined labels (Lee et al., 2024). This approach has also been applied to guardrail systems, where fine-tuned BERT models have been used to detect prompt injection or jailbreaks (Dubey and et al., 2024; Microsoft Corporation, 2024). These models are then commonly implemented within LLM guardrails such as LLM-Guard and Azure AI Content Safety (LLM Guard, 2025; Microsoft Corporation, 2024).
>
> **ZH**: **自然语言处理（NLP）分类技术**：在众多领域中，文本分类任务传统上依赖 NLP 判别模型将输入划分为预定义的类别标签 (Lee et al., 2024)。这种范式同样被广泛移植到护栏系统中，微调后的 BERT 类判别模型被普遍用于检测提示注入或越狱行为 (Dubey et al., 2024; Microsoft Corporation, 2024)。这些分类器随后被集成到主流 LLM 护栏产品中，如 LLM-Guard 和微软 Azure AI Content Safety 等 (LLM Guard, 2025; Microsoft Corporation, 2024)。

#### 2.1 LLM 安全威胁 / 2.1 LLM Threats

> **EN**: In this work we investigate threats that LLM guardrail systems are designed to protect against. Prompt injections are adversarial inputs crafted to induce the model to follow unintended instructions (Liu et al., 2024b). Jailbreaks, on the other hand, are prompts specifically designed to bypass the model’s safeguards and model training (Liu et al., 2024a). While the boundary between these attack vectors can be ambiguous, we treat them as distinct threat models in this work.
>
> **ZH**: 本文深入研究了护栏系统旨在防御的核心威胁。**提示注入（Prompt Injections）**是对抗性输入，旨在诱导模型执行非预期的指令 (Liu et al., 2024b)；而**越狱攻击（Jailbreaks）**则是专门用于突破模型内置安全防护与对齐训练的恶意提示词 (Liu et al., 2024a)。尽管这两个攻击向量在实际边界上有时存在交叠，但在本研究中，我们将它们视为两个独立且具有不同特征的威胁模型分别展开评测。

#### 2.2 威胁模型 / 2.2 Threat Model

> **EN**: We consider two threat models based on the level of access to the LLM guardrails. Black-box targets are systems that only provide a classification label or block the request when a malicious prompt is detected. We assume that access to these targets can be attained via API endpoints without rate limits or query restrictions. White-box targets, provide additional information such as confidence scores or logits, allowing attackers to carry out more effective attacks. White-box targets are accessed by downloading open-source models used by the target, either identified through documentation or publicly available information. The attackers goal across both threat models is to successfully evade correct classification.
>
> **ZH**: 我们根据攻击者对 LLM 护栏的访问权限级别构建了两种威胁模型：
> - **黑盒目标（Black-box targets）**：目标系统仅返回分类结果标签，或在检测到恶意提示词时直接阻断请求。我们假设攻击者可以通过 API 端点无速率限制或无查询阻拦地访问此类系统。
> - **白盒目标（White-box targets）**：目标系统能够提供额外的判别置信度分数（Confidence Scores）或未归一化对数几率（Logits），使攻击者能够施展高效的梯度或置信度引导攻击。攻击者通常通过下载该目标采用的开源模型权重获得白盒访问能力。
> - **攻击者终极目标**：在这两种威胁模型下，攻击者的目标均是在保留原有恶意攻击效用的同时，成功逃避护栏的正确恶意分类判决。

#### 2.3 目标护栏系统 / 2.3 Target Guardrails

> **EN**: We target 6 prominent prompt injection and jailbreak guardrails systems. We assume white-box access to all detectors except Azure Prompt Shield:
> - **Azure Prompt Shield**: Azure offer a LLM guardrail called Azure AI Content Safety which safeguards LLMs against malicious content. The system includes two types of guardrails - an ensemble of neural multi-class classification models for detecting content containing hate-speech, and violence, and a classification model known as Prompt Shield that protects deployed LLMs from two types of attacks: direct (jailbreaks), and indirect (prompt injections) (Microsoft Corporation, 2024). Prompt shield only returns a classification label if a detection has occurred, therefore we consider it as black-box target.
> - **ProtectAI Prompt Injection Detection v1 & v2**: ProtectAI proposed two open-source prompt injection models - v1 released 25th November 2023, and v2 on the 21st April 2024 (ProtectAI, 2023, 2024). Both models are fine-tuned from DeBERTa-v3-base (184m parameters) (He et al., 2021). We note that v2 specifies it isn’t trained to detect Jailbreak prompts, and therefore will not be evaluated on this threat.
> - **Meta Prompt Guard**: Prompt Guard is a multi-label classifier created by Meta which is designed to detect direct jailbreaks, or indirect prompt injections (Dubey and et al., 2024). We combined two of these categories—direct jailbreak and indirect prompt injection—into one, reducing the classification boundaries to a binary task. The model is fine-tuned from mDeBERTa-v3-base, a small (86M parameters) (He et al., 2021).
> - **NeMo Guard Jailbreak Detect**: NeMo Guard is a lightweight random forest-based jailbreak classifier developed by Nvidia, which utilizes pre-trained embedding pairs to identify jailbreaks (Galinkin and Sablotny, 2024).
> - **Vijil Prompt Injection**: Vijil Prompt Injection is a binary classifier designed to detect prompt injections aimed at manipulating or provoking harmful or unintended responses from an LLM (Vijil, 2025). The model was fine-tuned from ModernBert (Warner et al., 2024).
>
> **ZH**: 我们选取了 6 个最具代表性的提示注入与越狱护栏系统作为攻击靶标。除 Azure Prompt Shield 为商业黑盒 API 外，其余检测器均具有白盒访问能力：
> 1. **Azure Prompt Shield**（微软商业护栏）：属于 Azure AI Content Safety 套件，集成多分类神经网络集成模型（检测仇恨言论、暴力等）与 Prompt Shield 模块，用于防御直接越狱与间接注入。由于 API 仅返回布尔型判定标签，我们将其严格作为纯黑盒目标评测。
> 2. **ProtectAI Prompt Injection Detection v1 & v2**：ProtectAI 推出的开源提示注入判别模型，均基于 DeBERTa-v3-base（1.84 亿参数）微调。v2 版本特别声明未对越狱提示词进行针对性训练，因此对 v2 仅测试提示注入。
> 3. **Meta Prompt Guard**：Meta 开发的多标签分类器，基于轻量级多语言判别器 mDeBERTa-v3-base（8600 万参数）微调，设计用于识别直接越狱与间接注入。我们将其输出映射为二分类任务。
> 4. **英伟达 NeMo Guard Jailbreak Detect**：基于预训练 Embedding 句向量对特征的随机森林（Random Forest）轻量级越狱分类器。
> 5. **Vijil Prompt Injection**：基于最新 ModernBERT 架构微调的高性能二分类判别模型，专门用于识别操纵或诱导 LLM 非预期响应的提示注入。

### 3 逃逸攻击技术 / 3 Evasion Techniques

> **EN**: Evasion attacks are a set of attacks which aim to evade correct classification by the target system (Biggio et al., 2013). We leverage two sets of evasion techniques against the LLM guardrails: Character Injection and Adversarial ML Evasion.
>
> **ZH**: 逃逸攻击（Evasion Attacks）是一类旨在规避目标系统正确分类决策的对抗攻击 (Biggio et al., 2013)。本文针对 LLM 护栏系统采用了两类逃避技术体系：**字符注入（Character Injection）**与**对抗机器学习逃避（Adversarial ML Evasion）**。

#### 3.1 字符注入技术 / 3.1 Character Injection

> **EN**: Character injection techniques are black-box methods used to manipulate and induce unexpected behavior in a system by injecting characters that the system fails to handle properly. These techniques are an established attack vector in cyber security and are commonly employed to perform exploits such as SQL injection and command injection (Sadeghian et al., 2013). In the context of AI models, character injection techniques have been demonstrated as a means of attacking NLP models and LLM guardrails (Boucher et al., 2021; Claburn, 2024). Since LLMs are capable of interpreting encoded and modified text, they can still comprehend and execute encoded prompt injection or jailbreak payloads, despite text obfuscation or alteration. We selected 12 character injection techniques as shown in Table 1.
>
> **ZH**: 字符注入技术是一种黑盒方法，通过注入目标系统无法正确处理或解析的特殊字符，诱发系统的非预期行为。该技术是网络安全中的经典向量，常用于 SQL 注入和命令注入攻击 (Sadeghian et al., 2013)。在 AI 模型语境下，字符注入已被证实可用于对抗 NLP 模型和 LLM 护栏 (Boucher et al., 2021; Claburn, 2024)。由于生成式大语言模型具备极强的语义解码与容错理解能力，即使输入文本遭受严重字符混淆或编码变形，底层 LLM 依然能够完整领会并顺从执行攻击有效载荷（Payload）。我们系统选取了如表 1 所示的 12 种字符注入技术。

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                表 1: 12 种字符注入技术与变换样例 (以 "Hello" 为例)                         |
+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+--------------------------+
| 注入技术名称               | 技术简要描述                                        | 变换输出示例 ("Hello")   |
```

#### 3.2 对抗机器学习逃逸 / 3.2 Adversarial ML Evasion

> **EN**: Adversarial ML (AML) Evasion techniques aim to modify input text to a black-box or white-box classifier by using different perturbation methods upon a computed list of word rankings. The technique’s aim is to highlight over reliance on learned features, blind spots within their training, whilst maintaining semantic similarity to the original text (Morris et al., 2020). The techniques explored within our work consist of two stages:
> - **(1) Word Importance Ranking**: For a given prompt, the attack generates a ranking of words based on their influence over the classifier’s decision. This ranking is derived using methods such as gradient-based techniques, word removal, and word saliency, which quantify each word’s contribution to the overall classification. The efficacy of the word importance ranking is related to the threat model access to the target.
> - **(2) Perturbation**: The ranked words are then modified maintaining their semantic meaning but disrupting the classifier’s ability to process them correctly. Perturbations include synonym substitution, introduction of typos, and reordering of words. The process is iterative, where after each perturbation, feedback from the model is used to refine the attack, gradually improving its effectiveness.
> Table 2 shows the 8 selected adversarial ML evasion techniques explored within this paper.
>
> **ZH**: 对抗机器学习（AML）逃避技术旨在根据计算出的**词重要度排序列表（Word Importance Ranking）**，采用多种扰动策略修改输入文本，以逃避黑盒或白盒判别分类器的识别。该技术的精髓在于揭示分类器对特定习得特征的过度依赖以及训练集中的盲区，同时严格保持扰动文本与原始提示词之间的高度语义相似性 (Morris et al., 2020)。本文探索的 AML 逃逸包含两个核心阶段：
> 1. **词重要度排序（Word Importance Ranking）**：针对给定提示词，攻击算法根据每个单词对分类器判决的影响力生成重要度排名。该排序通过基于梯度（Gradient-based）、词移除消融（Word Removal）或显著性分析（Word Saliency）等方法量化每个单词对恶意判别的边际贡献率。
> 2. **词级扰动（Perturbation）**：按照重要度排名，依次对关键单词施加微扰——在保持整体语义通顺的前提下破坏分类器对其特征的抓取。扰动形式包括同义词替换、拼写微错（Typos）以及词序微调等。该过程是一个迭代反馈循环：每次微扰后，根据模型返回的反馈信号进一步精细化调整，逐步逼近最佳规避状态。

### 4 实验设置 / 4 Experimental Setup

> **EN**: Guardrail Setup. All guardrails were accessed via an API endpoint, returning the top classification label, and in the event of white-box guardrails, the confidence values, with only Azure Prompt Shield omitting confidence values due to being black-box and hosted upon Azure (See Section 2). Guardrails were deployed for GPT-4o-mini leveraging each of the evaluated LLM guardrails before inputs are passed to the LLM.
> Evasion Techniques. Character injection techniques were applied via an automated system which modified a given text input using Unicode characters (e.g., zero-width characters, homoglyphs) or character smuggling techniques that obfuscate input as perceived by classifiers (Wei et al., 2025). In contrast, AML evasion techniques were implemented via TextAttack - an open-source library for generating adversarial examples for NLP models (Morris et al., 2020). For both methods, perturbations are applied to each dataset sample, and detection is evaluated pre- and post-attack. Attack Success Rate (ASR) is defined as the rate at which a modified prompt injection or jailbreak sample is misclassified as benign.
> Evasion Setup. To demonstrate the effectiveness of the evasion techniques, we selected two datasets. The first is a prompt injection dataset called safe-guard-prompt-injection, consisting of 10,296 prompt injection and benign examples (Erdogan et al., 2024). From its test set (2,060 examples), we selected only adversarial samples (650 examples), finally filtering out jailbreak samples totaling 476 prompt injection prompts. For jailbreaks, we used an open-source repository containing 78 prompts (NoDataFound, 2024). We evaluated various guardrail baselines on their ability to detect these two categories of adversarial prompts, with the resulting detection rates shown in Table A.1.
>
> **ZH**:
> - **护栏系统部署环境**：所有护栏均通过标准的 API 端点进行交互；对于白盒护栏，API 会返回分类判据及置信度数值；而微软 Azure Prompt Shield 因部署于 Azure 云端黑盒环境，仅返回判决标签。所有护栏均部署在下游模型 **GPT-4o-mini** 的前端，在用户输入提示词到达核心大模型前先由护栏进行串联阻断。
> - **逃逸技术执行框架**：字符注入技术通过自动化管线执行，向输入文本中系统性嵌入 Unicode 异常字符（如零宽空格、同形字符）或走私技术；AML 逃避算法则依托于 NLP 对抗攻击权威开源框架 **TextAttack** (Morris et al., 2020)。针对两个数据集中的样本，分别测量扰动前后的检测率变化。**攻击成功率（Attack Success Rate, ASR）**严格定义为：**经过扰动的恶意提示注入或越狱样本被护栏误判为良性（Benign/Safe）的比例**。
> - **实验数据集选取**：
>   1. **提示注入基准数据集**：选取 `safe-guard-prompt-injection` 数据集 (Erdogan et al., 2024)，总含 10,296 条提示注入与良性样本。从测试集（2,060 条）中过滤提取出 650 条对抗性样本，进一步剔除混合越狱样本后，最终精选出 **476 条纯净提示注入测试提示词**。
>   2. **越狱攻击基准数据集**：采用开源对抗仓库提供的 **78 条典型越狱提示词** (NoDataFound, 2024)。各大护栏在此两大原始未扰动基准上的基线检测准确率详见附录表 A.1。

#### 5.1 字符注入实证结果 / 5.1 Character Injection

> **EN**: Figure 2 and 3 shows the results across datasets, character injection techniques and LLM guardrails.
> Guardrail Resilience. Across all evaluated models, Vijil Prompt Injection exhibited the highest susceptibility, with average ASRs of 87.95% for prompt injections and 91.67% for jailbreaks. Protect AI v1 followed, yielding 77.32% and 51.39% respectively. NeMo Guard Jailbreak Detect recorded an ASR of 72.54% upon jailbreaks. Azure Prompt Shield was bypassed with average ASRs of 71.98% for prompt injections and 60.15% for jailbreaks, while Meta Prompt Guard demonstrated similar susceptibility, with ASRs of 70.44% and 73.08%. In contrast, Protect AI v2 showed marked improvement over its predecessor, reducing the prompt injection ASR to 20.26%, and only heavily bypassed by Emoji and Unicode tag Smuggling.
> Attack Effectiveness. Character injection techniques demonstrated a high degree of effectiveness in evading detection. The most successful attack was Emoji Smuggling, which achieved a 100% ASR for both prompt injections and jailbreaks, followed by Upside Down achieved 100% ASR for Jailbreaks. Unicode Tags followed closely, with ASRs of 90.15% and 81.79%, respectively. Several other attacks also proved highly effective, including Numbers (81.18% / 94.62%), Bidirectional Text (78.69% / 99.23%), and Upside Down Text (63.54% / 100%). Notably, attacks such as Diacritics, Homoglyphs, Zero-Width Characters, Underline Accent Marks, and Full Width Text consistently evaded with moderate success, yielding average ASRs between 44–76% across datasets. The least effective technique was Deletion Characters, with ASRs of 26.82% for prompt injections and 7.95% for jailbreaks. These results suggest significant variance in the susceptibility of models to different character perturbations due to differences in tokenizer training exposure to adversarial text and encoding strategies (Boucher et al., 2021).
>

> - **字符注入技术的攻击效能对比**：
>   - **Emoji Smuggling（表情走私）**斩获全场最高效能：在提示注入和越狱攻击两大数据集上均达成了 **100% 完美的攻击成功率（ASR = 100%）**！
>   - **Upside Down Text（倒立字）**在越狱测试中同样夺得 100% ASR。
>   - **Unicode 标签走私（Unicode Tags）**同样极具毁灭性，两项 ASR 分别高达 90.15% 与 81.79%。
>   - **Numbers（数字同形替换）**（81.18% / 94.62%）与 **Bidirectional Text（双向排版文本）**（78.69% / 99.23%）亦展现出惊人的穿透率。
>   - 变音符（Diacritics）、同形字（Homoglyphs）、零宽字符（Zero-Width）、下划重音以及全角字符（Full Width）在各大护栏上表现出稳健的中高攻击效力（平均 ASR 在 44%~76% 之间）。

### 6 深度讨论 / 6 Discussion

#### 6.1 护栏逃逸成功之成因 / 6.1 Guardrail Evasion Success

> **EN**: Character injection techniques have demonstrated to be highly effective while requiring minimal effort from adversaries. Interestingly, smuggling techniques such as emoji, and unicode tags emerged as effective injections, while other techniques varied in success suggesting that target LLM guardrails can differ in susceptibility to this type of evasion. This points to weaknesses in the underlying model architecture or training process. The effectiveness of these attacks likely varies depending on the training data each model has been exposed to, emphasizing the differences in learned behavior and susceptibility across different targets (Wei et al., 2025). Models trained on diverse datasets or those with better generalized understanding are typically more resistant, while others remain vulnerable due to the specific content they’ve encountered during training.
> Adversarial ML evasion techniques are particularly effective in white-box models, where attackers have access to confidence values allowing adversaries to craft highly precise and targeted perpetuated samples that can bypass correct classification. In contrast, attacking black-box models, where output information is limited, require more time and effort (Li et al., 2019). The lack of confidence values forces adversaries to rely on trial-and-error, running attacks for longer periods and with less certainty of success. Despite these challenges, these attacks reveal significant vulnerabilities in model guardrails, showing how blind spots in training can be exploited to produce imperceptible prompt injections and jailbreaks that evade detection.
>
> **ZH**:
> - **字符注入为何如此高效率**：字符注入技术在仅需极小对抗成本的前提下展现出了极高穿透力。尤其是 Emoji 走私和 Unicode 标签走私技术几乎呈现全线击穿之势。这直接指向了现有判别模型底层架构与预训练数据的先天缺陷：模型的抵抗能力高度取决于分词器在预训练语料中是否充分见过此类非常规字符。缺乏泛化数据支撑的判别器在遇到未登录编码时，往往将其直接切分为无意义碎片或丢弃，从而导致原本强烈的恶意判别特征被彻底抹除。
> - **AML 逃逸与黑白盒鸿沟**：算法化 AML 逃避在白盒场景下极具毁灭性，攻击者依托连续的置信度信号能够以极低扰动预算快速收敛出不可察觉的对抗样本；而在黑盒商业 API 场景下，由于缺乏置信度反馈，攻击者原本只能陷入反复试错的低效盲搜。但一旦结合了跨模型迁移机制，黑盒护栏的假象便被彻底击穿。

#### 6.2 词重要度跨模型迁移机理 / 6.2 Word Importance Transferability

> **EN**: As presented in Section 5.3, we observed that attack transferability can increase the ASR across multiple attack techniques (Table 3). By using a white-box model to compute word selection, the generated perturbations are more effective when launched against black-box targets. This highlights the potential for adversarial transferability to bridge the gap between white-box and black-box attack scenarios, enhancing their attack strategies when limited output information is provided. By refining perturbations on a white-box model that closely approximates the black-box system, adversaries are capable of developing more effective attacks against LLM guardrails.
>
> **ZH**: 实证表明，利用开源白盒模型计算词重要度排序，能够实质性打破黑盒目标的信息封锁。由于当代用于安全检测的判别分类器（如 DeBERTa、BERT、ModernBERT、RoBERTa）在语法注意力结构与对恶意指令语义的表征特征上具有共通性，高影响力的“触发词（Trigger Words）”在不同模型间呈现出极高的重合度。攻击者在白盒代理模型上精准标定并微调这些核心触发词后，迁移至闭源黑盒系统同样能产生高度一致的误判效应。这提示我们：**防御方绝不能将安全性建立在“仅返回布尔标签”的黑盒隐蔽性（Security through Obscurity）假设之上**。

#### 6.3 护栏与底层 LLM 的输入理解割裂 / 6.3 Guardrails and LLM Input Differences

> **EN**: The relationship between guardrails and LLMs reveal interesting differences in how they handle inputs. LLM Guardrails can be trained on entirely different datasets than the underlying LLM, resulting in their inability to detect certain character injection techniques that the LLM itself can understand. As shown in Section 5.1, character injection techniques can completely evade guardrail detection. This poses a risk because inputs that bypass the guardrails may still be properly interpreted by the LLM (Claburn, 2024). In addition to differences in training data, guardrails may also have inherent design differences—such as limited input size and token support—that can be exploited to further evade classification (Wei et al., 2025). These limitations highlight a critical weakness in current guardrail implementations and demonstrate a further need to understand how inputs could be crafted to intentionally bypass guardrails while remaining fully comprehensible to the LLM.
>
> **ZH**: **护栏与底层生成式 LLM 之间的“输入处理代差”是导致系统沦陷的最根本根源**。在典型系统架构中，前端护栏（通常是轻量级判别模型，如 86M 的 mDeBERTa 或 184M 的 DeBERTa）与后端核心 LLM（如百亿/千亿参数的 GPT-4、Llama-3）是由完全不同的团队、基于完全不同的预训练语料和分词器构建的：
> 1. **分词与理解能力的不对称性**：当前端护栏遇到字符注入（如全角字符、颠倒字、Emoji 标签、Leetspeak）时，其简化的分词器无法识别这些词元，判定特征被彻底打散，从而给出了“良性（Safe）”的错误放行结论；
> 2. **下游 LLM 的强鲁棒执行**：然而，后端的生成式基础大模型拥有极其海量的上下文推断和容错还原能力，能够瞬间识别并完美读懂被混淆的真实意图，进而毫无阻碍地执行有害指令！
> 这种“护栏眼瞎而 LLM 洞若观火”的结构性矛盾，使得攻击者能够极其轻易地构造出“既能骗过护栏、又能令 LLM 彻底破防”的特制对抗输入。

### 7 结论 / 7 Conclusion

> **EN**: In this paper we have conducted an empirical analysis of the effectiveness of LLM guardrail systems to detect jailbreak and prompt injection when exposed to evasion attacks. Our research uncovers vulnerabilities within current LLM guardrails, identifying two primary attack vectors: Character injection and Adversarial Machine Learning (AML) evasion techniques. Character injection methods, such as emoji smuggling and bidirectional text, enable near-complete evasion of some guardrails with minimal effort. In contrast, AML techniques demonstrate effective, imperceptible evasion by exploiting training blind spots. Furthermore, we demonstrate that attackers can use white-box models to enhance evasion effectiveness against black-box targets. These findings highlight critical weaknesses in existing defenses and emphasize the need for more robust LLM guardrails.
>
> **ZH**: 本文针对 LLM 护栏系统在面对逃避攻击时检测越狱与提示注入的有效性展开了深度实证分析。我们的研究揭示了现有 LLM 护栏中潜藏的深层安全漏洞，系统标定了两大核心攻击向量：**字符注入技术**与**对抗机器学习（AML）逃避技术**。字符注入方法（如表情走私、双向文本等）能以极低的对抗代价实现对部分主流护栏接近 100% 的彻底攻破；而 AML 算法技术则通过精准利用模型的训练盲区，实现了高效且肉眼不可察觉的逃避伪装。此外，我们证明了攻击者能够利用离线白盒模型跨模型显著推高对黑盒商业目标的攻击穿透力。这些发现有力揭示了当前防护机制的关键软肋，为构建下一代更具鲁棒性、多层次深度防御的 LLM 安全护栏系统提供了至关重要的实证基石与设计警示。

### 8 局限性 / 8 Limitations

> **EN**:
> - **Black-box Target Scope**: Our study focused solely on Azure Prompt Shield as the representative black-box target. While this allowed us to evaluate the effectiveness of our techniques in a realistic commercial setting, it limits the generalizability of our findings. Future research should investigate a broader range of commercial systems and defense mechanisms to assess the robustness and adaptability of the proposed methods in diverse environments.
> - **Further Transferability Work**: Our work demonstrates that using white-box models can improve the effectiveness of attacks against black-box systems. However, the underlying mechanisms driving this transferability, particularly regarding word importance, remain unclear. More research is needed to understand the semantic and architectural factors that influence transferability between models, which could inform both attack strategies and defense design.
> - **Adversarial Prompt Efficacy**: We used various perturbation techniques to evade detection or filtering that may impact the underlying efficacy of the original prompts. Although we conducted our own evaluations to assess the functionality of perturbed prompts, more rigorous quantitative analyses are needed to determine how perturbations affect the success rate and intended behavior of modified prompt injections or jailbreaks.
>
> **ZH**:
> - **黑盒商业评测对象的范围局限**：本研究在黑盒方面仅以微软 Azure Prompt Shield 作为唯一的商用代表。尽管这让我们得以在真实商业生产环境中评估技术的有效性，但其普适性结论有待扩展。未来研究需要进一步覆盖 AWS Bedrock Guardrails、Google Cloud Model Armor 等更多主流商业防护网。
> - **迁移性内在机理有待深化**：尽管本文证实了白盒模型能够赋能黑盒规避，但驱动这一可迁移性的深层几何机制（尤其是词重要度在不同注意力层间的流动与重合）尚未完全厘清，未来需结合注意力图谱与表征几何学进一步深入探索。
> - **对抗提示词的语义效用量化**：尽管我们进行了定性验证以确保扰动后的提示词依然能促使下游模型生成预期攻击行为，但未来仍需建立更严格、规模化的大样本量化基准，系统测量各种微扰对指令遵循率与越狱危害度的边际影响。

### 针对 M3_PI_Guard 的防御启示与核心总结

1. **单纯依靠外挂判别式分类器（Guardrails）存在致命的“理解代差”漏洞**：
   - 判别模型（如 DeBERTa/ModernBERT）参数量小、分词器词表狭窄，极易被简单的 Unicode 变体、Emoji 走私、不可见字符彻底致盲；
   - 而下游生成式 LLM（如 GPT-4、Claude、Qwen、Llama）参数巨大、容错极强，依然能够正确执行被混淆的恶意载荷。
2. **“安全通过隐蔽性（Security through Obscurity）”在白盒迁移面前不堪一击**：
   - 商业 API 即使不返回置信度、仅返回布尔型阻断标签，攻击者只要利用本地开源白盒模型离线计算词重要度分布，即可直接迁移攻破黑盒防御。
3. **M3_PI_Guard 架构的先进性与启示**：
   - 不能仅在“输入阶段”做一次性文本分类拦截，必须引入**多阶段执行契约（Execution Contracts）**与**提交前工具流审计（Verify-Before-Commit / Tool Stream Guard）**；
   - 必须在分词前端对输入进行严格的 Unicode 规范化清洗（Unicode Normalization NFKC）、不可见字符剥离与同形字折叠；
   - 结合基于权限隔离的不可行策略编译，无论提示词如何逃避前端文本检测，一旦在运行时试图触发越权敏感操作，均在底层系统调用与工具执行层被强制掐断。
