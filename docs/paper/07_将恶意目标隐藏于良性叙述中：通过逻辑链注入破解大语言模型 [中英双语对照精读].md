### 摘要 / Abstract

> **EN**: Jailbreak attacks on Language Model Models (LLMs) entail crafting prompts aimed at exploiting the models to generate malicious content. Existing jailbreak attacks can successfully deceive the LLMs, however they cannot deceive the human. This paper proposes a new type of jailbreak attacks which can deceive both the LLMs and human (i.e., security analyst). The key insight of our idea is borrowed from the social psychology – that is human are easily deceived if the lie is hidden in truth. Based on this insight, we proposed the logic-chain injection attacks to inject malicious intention into benign truth. Logic-chain injection attack firstly dissembles its malicious target into a chain of benign narrations, and then distribute narrations into a related benign article, with undoubted facts. In this way, newly generate prompt cannot only deceive the LLMs, but also deceive human.
> Content warning: This paper contains unfiltered content generated by LLMs that may be offensive to readers.
>
> **ZH**: 针对大语言模型（LLM）的越狱攻击涉及精心构建提示词，旨在诱导模型生成恶意内容。现有的越狱攻击能够成功欺骗大语言模型，但无法瞒过人类审查。本文提出了一种新型越狱攻击，能够同时欺骗大语言模型和人类审查者（即安全分析人员）。我们核心构想的灵感源于社会心理学——即如果谎言隐藏在事实之中，人类便极易受骗。基于这一洞察，我们提出了**逻辑链注入攻击（Logic-Chain Injection Attacks）**，将恶意意图注入良性事实之中。逻辑链注入攻击首先将恶意目标拆解为一条由良性叙述构成的链条，随后将这些叙述分散嵌入到具有无可争议事实的同主题良性文章中。通过这种方式，新生成的提示词不仅能够欺骗大语言模型，也能瞒过人类审查。

### 1 引言 / 1 Introduction

> **EN**: Large Language Models (LLMs) such as BERT [6] (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) by Devlin et al. and GPT [11] (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) by Radford et al., have revolutionized the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) with their exceptional capabilities, setting new standards in performance across various tasks. Due to their superb generative capability, LLMs are widely deployed as the backend for various real-world applications, referred to as LLM-Integrated Applications. For instance, Microsoft utilizes GPT-4 as the service backend for the new Bing Search [1]; OpenAI has developed various applications—such as ChatWithPDF and AskTheCode—that utilize GPT-4 for different tasks such as text processing, code interpretation, and product recommendation [2, 3]; Google deploys the search engine Bard, powered by PaLM 2.
>
> **ZH**: 大语言模型（LLM），如 Devlin 等人提出的 BERT [6]（基于 Transformers 的双向编码器表示）以及 Radford 等人提出的 GPT [11]（生成式预训练 Transformer），凭借其卓越的能力彻底变革了自然语言处理（NLP）领域，在各项任务中树立了全新的性能基准。得益于其出色的生成能力，大语言模型被广泛部署为各类实际应用的后台支撑，这类应用被称为大语言模型集成应用（LLM-Integrated Applications）。例如，微软将 GPT-4 用作新 Bing 搜索的服务后台 [1]；OpenAI 开发了 ChatWithPDF 和 AskTheCode 等多种应用，利用 GPT-4 执行文本处理、代码解释和产品推荐等不同任务 [2, 3]；谷歌部署了由 PaLM 2 驱动的 Bard 搜索引擎。

> **EN**: In general, to accomplish a task, an LLM-Integrated Application requires an instruction prompt, which aims to instruct the backend LLM to perform the task, and a data prompt, which is the data to be processed by the LLM in the task. The instruction prompt can be provided by a user or the LLM-Integrated Application itself; and the data prompt is often obtained from external resources such as emails and webpages on the Internet. An LLM-Integrated Application queries the backend LLM using the instruction prompt and data prompt to accomplish the task and returns the response from the LLM to the user.
>
> **ZH**: 通常而言，为了完成一项任务，大语言模型集成应用需要指令提示词（用于指导后台大语言模型执行任务）以及数据提示词（即任务中由大语言模型处理的数据）。指令提示词可由用户或大语言模型集成应用本身提供；而数据提示词通常获取自外部资源，例如互联网上的电子邮件和网页。大语言模型集成应用使用指令提示词和数据提示词查询后台大语言模型以完成任务，并将大语言模型的响应返回给用户。

> **EN**: Recently, several types of vulnerabilities have been identified in LLMs to deceive models or mislead users. Among these, prompt injection attacks and jailbreak attacks stand out as prevalent vulnerabilities. Prompt injection attacks were proposed to mislead users of LLM-Integrated Applications [10, 8, 7, 15, 5, 9]. Prompt injections involve bypassing filters or manipulating the LLM using carefully crafted prompts that make the model ignore previous instructions or perform unintended actions. These vulnerabilities can lead to unintended consequences, including data leakage, unauthorized access, or other security breaches. In jailbreak attacks [13], specially designed prompts are used to bypass the safety censorship of LLMs, facilitating the production of harmful content.
>
> **ZH**: 近期，研究人员在大语言模型中发现了若干可用于欺骗模型或误导用户的安全漏洞。其中，提示注入攻击和越狱攻击尤为突出，属于最为普遍的脆弱性威胁。提示注入攻击的提出旨在误导大语言模型集成应用的用户 [10, 8, 7, 15, 5, 9]。提示注入涉及绕过过滤器或使用精心设计的提示词操纵大语言模型，使模型忽略先前的指令或执行非预期的操作。这些漏洞可能导致非预期的严重后果，包括数据泄露、未授权访问或其他安全入侵。在越狱攻击中 [13]，攻击者使用专门设计的提示词绕过大语言模型的安全审查，从而促成有害内容的生成。

> **EN**: However, we found that the existing methods directly embed/inject their intention into the prompt, which can be easily detected through input permutation defenses, self-processing defenses, and additional helper defenses. These defenses essentially pre-process the data prompt to remove the injected instruction/data of the injected task and/or re-design the instruction prompt. Most importantly, all these kinds of attacks are easily identified by human, which proves that they are all far from the intelligence of mankind.
>
> **ZH**: 然而，我们发现现有方法均直接将恶意意图嵌入/注入到提示词中，这极易被输入排列防御、自处理防御以及辅助助手防御所检测。这些防御手段本质上是对数据提示词进行预处理，以消除所注入任务的指令/数据，和/或重新设计指令提示词。最重要的是，所有这类攻击都极易被人类识别，这证明它们与人类的智慧仍相距甚远。

> **EN**: To achieve a more sophisticated attack, we adopt the strategies that adopted by human to deceive others. We observed that in social psychology a clever liar tends to hide lies into truth and sophisticated software attacks (i.e., ROP attack [12]) reuse the benign code in program space to achieve their malicious purpose. Looking back at the history of software attacks, in the early stage of software security, shell-code injection was a popular attack method which directly injected malicious instructions into the target program and diverted execution to this piece of code. Later on, this kind of exploitation are prevented by the non-executable data policy, which prevents execution of the injected shell-code. To bypass the non-executable data policy, one of the smartest attackers proposed the idea of reusing existing code pieces (gadgets) in program space to achieve their malicious goal. Such kinds of attacks include Return-to-Libc and Return-Oriented Programming (ROP). The reused code can be an existing function or a small piece of instruction sequences (gadgets) found anywhere in the code that can be chained together to carry out malicious operations.
>
> **ZH**: 为了实现更为隐蔽精密的攻击，我们借鉴了人类欺骗他人的策略。我们观察到，在社会心理学中，狡猾的骗子往往倾向于将谎言隐匿于真实之中；而精密的软件攻击（如 ROP 攻击 [12]）则重用程序空间中的良性代码来实现恶意目的。回顾软件攻击的历史，在软件安全的早期阶段，shellcode 注入是一种流行的攻击方法，它直接向目标程序中注入恶意指令并将执行流重定向至该代码段。随后，不可执行数据策略（DEP/NX）阻止了此类利用，防止了注入 shellcode 的执行。为了绕过不可执行数据策略，最聪明的攻击者提出了重用程序空间中现有代码片段（gadgets）来实现恶意目标的思路。此类攻击包括 Return-to-Libc 和面向返回编程（ROP）。所重用的代码可以是现有函数，也可以是在代码任意位置找到的一小组指令序列（gadgets），它们可以被链接在一起以执行恶意操作。

> **EN**: Based on the key insights, we proposed a more advanced method (Logic-Chain Injection Attacks) to inject malicious intention into benign truth. For instance, consider the process of constructing a prompt to inject malicious content: Begin with a malicious query Q for LLM, our attack first disassembles the malicious query Q into a sequence of semantic equivalent (=∥=) narrations. Secondly, we distribute the logic-chain of narrations into different location of a benign article of the similar topic. We propose logic-chain distribution method so that LLM can connect the scattered logic while the logic chain is decentralized. Figure 1 shows an example.
>
> **ZH**: 基于这些核心洞察，我们提出了一种更为先进的方法（**逻辑链注入攻击，Logic-Chain Injection Attacks**），将恶意意图注入良性真实内容中。例如，考虑构建用于注入恶意内容的提示词的过程：首先针对大语言模型设定一个恶意查询 $Q$，我们的攻击首先将恶意查询 $Q$ 拆解为一系列语义等价（$=\parallel=$）的叙述。其次，我们将叙述构成的逻辑链分散嵌入到相似主题良性文章的不同位置。我们提出了逻辑链分布方法，使得大语言模型在逻辑链去中心化的同时能够连接离散的逻辑。图 1 展示了一个示例。

Unlike existing attacks, which follow specific prompt patterns to inject malicious intentions into prompts, the logic-chain injection attack does not follow any specific patterns, making it hard to be detected. Specifically, the logic-chain injection attack injects useful gadget sentences from the instruction prompt or data prompt, and then chains their logic together to inject malicious intention. Even humans cannot detect such kinds of attacks without careful review.
>

### 2 背景 / 2 Background

#### 2.1 大语言模型越狱攻击 / 2.1 LLM Jailbreak Attack

> **EN**: Jailbreak attacks on Language Model Models (LLMs) entail crafting prompts aimed at exploiting the models to generate malicious content. Despite the grave potential consequences, such as generating instructions for fabricating explosives, LLMs typically abstain from producing such responses owing to the integration of various safeguards implemented during their training process.
> “Benign content” is defined as responses considered morally or ethically inappropriate, with OpenAI compiling an extensive list of such categories. Liu et al. [9] further elaborate on this classification, providing a framework for categorizing these responses. The assessment presented herein conforms to this established categorization, ensuring a structured approach to understanding and mitigating jailbreak risks in LLMs. Based on the literature, existing Jailbreak attacks can be classified into the following categories:
> - **Prompt-Level Jailbreaks** aim to use semantically meaningful deception and social engineering to elicit objectionable content from LLMs. While effective, this technique requires creativity, manual dataset curation, and customized human feedback, leading to considerable human time and resource investments [5]. Template-based methods are typical cases of prompt-level jailbreaks.
> - **Token-Level Jailbreaks** involve optimizing the set of tokens received by a targeted LLM as input. While this class of jailbreaks has received recognition for its efficacy, such attacks require hundreds of thousands of queries—being more computationally expensive than prompt-level jailbreaks—and are often uninterpretable to humans [5]. For example, the adversarial suffix method appends an adversarial suffix to the query that attempts to induce negative behavior.
> - **Generative Jailbreaks** dynamically produce attacks. For example, the gradient-guided token-level search method (automatic attacks like GCG) searches for the prompt that can successfully evade the LLM guided by the gradient information on tokens. Although it provides a way to automatically generate jailbreak prompts, this leads to an intrinsic drawback: they often generate jailbreak prompts composed of nonsensical sequences or gibberish, i.e., without any semantic meaning [8]. To automate the generation of jailbreak templates for LLMs, GPTfuzz starts with human-written templates. It uses a series of random mutations to generate new inputs and evaluates them with the assistance of LLMs. The adversary is assumed to have black-box access to the LLMs [15].
> - **Obfuscation-Based Jailbreaks** represent a systematic and intentional approach that uses some non-English translation or obfuscation to jailbreak the LLMs. Given an input, it is translated from English into another language, fed into GPT-4, and subsequently translated back into English. The key insight behind this kind of attack is that safety mechanisms do not generalize to low-resource languages due to the limited resources during the model training/deployment phase [14].
> - **Parameter-Based Method** is an approach that disrupts model alignment by only manipulating variations of decoding methods [7]. It manages to jailbreak the target LLM by exploiting different generation strategies, including varying decoding hyperparameters and sampling methods, instead of manipulating jailbreak prompts. Thus, the adversary is assumed to have white-box access to the LLMs [7].
>
> **ZH**: 针对大语言模型（LLM）的越狱攻击涉及精心设计提示词，旨在诱导模型生成恶意内容。尽管可能产生制造爆炸物指南等严重后果，但大语言模型通常因其训练过程中集成的各项防护机制而拒绝生成此类响应。  
> “不良内容”被界定为在道德或伦理上被视为不当的响应，OpenAI 汇总了详尽的类别清单。Liu 等人 [9] 进一步阐述了这一分类，为对这些响应进行归类提供了系统框架。本文的评估遵循这一既定分类标准，从而确保采用结构化方法来理解和缓解大语言模型中的越狱风险。根据现有文献，现有越狱攻击可划分为以下类别：  
> - **提示词级越狱（Prompt-Level Jailbreaks）**：旨在利用具有语义含义的欺骗手段和社会工程学诱导大语言模型输出不良内容。尽管该技术有效，但它需要创造力、人工数据集构建以及定制化的人工反馈，消耗相当多的人力与资源投入 [5]。基于模板的方法是提示词级越狱的典型代表。  
> - **标记级越狱（Token-Level Jailbreaks）**：涉及优化目标大语言模型作为输入接收的标记集合。尽管此类越狱因其有效性而受到认可，但此类攻击需要数十万次查询——在计算开销上远高于提示词级越狱——且往往对人类不可解释 [5]。例如，对抗性后缀方法在查询末尾追加一个对抗性后缀，试图诱发负面行为。  
> - **生成式越狱（Generative Jailbreaks）**：动态生成攻击。例如，梯度引导的标记级搜索方法（如 GCG 等自动化攻击）在标记梯度的引导下搜索能够成功规避大语言模型安全对齐的提示词。尽管它提供了一种自动生成越狱提示词的途径，但这带来了一个固有缺陷：它们生成的越狱提示词往往由无意义的序列或乱码组成，即缺乏任何语义含义 [8]。为了自动化生成大语言模型的越狱模板，GPTfuzz 从人工编写的模板出发，利用一系列随机变异生成新输入，并在大语言模型的辅助下进行评估。攻击者被假设对大语言模型拥有黑盒访问权限 [15]。  
> - **基于混淆的越狱（Obfuscation-Based Jailbreaks）**：代表了一种系统且故意的途径，利用某些非英语翻译或混淆手段来破解大语言模型。给定一个输入，将其从英文翻译成另一种语言，输入给 GPT-4，随后再翻译回英文。此类攻击背后的核心洞察在于，由于模型训练/部署阶段资源有限，安全机制无法泛化到低资源语言 [14]。  
> - **基于参数的方法（Parameter-Based Method）**：是一种仅通过操纵解码方法的变体来破坏模型对齐的方法 [7]。它通过利用不同的生成策略（包括改变解码超参数和采样方法）来成功越狱目标大语言模型，而不是操纵越狱提示词。因此，攻击者被假设对大语言模型拥有白盒访问权限 [7]。

### 3 逻辑链注入攻击 / 3 Logic-Chain Injection Attack

#### 3.1 形式化定义 / 3.1 Formalization

> **EN**: We reuse the formalization of the Jailbreak from [8] to define our attacking method. Specifically, given a sequence of tokens $\langle x_1, x_2, \dots, x_m angle$, the LLM estimates the probability distribution over the vocabulary for the next token $x_{m+1}$:
> $$x_{m+j} \sim P(\cdot \mid x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{m+j-1}), \quad 	ext{for } j = 1, 2, \dots, k \quad (1)$$
>
> **ZH**: 我们复用 [8] 中的越狱形式化体系来定义我们的攻击方法。具体而言，给定一个标记序列 $\langle x_1, x_2, \dots, x_m angle$，大语言模型对词表中下一个标记 $x_{m+1}$ 的概率分布进行估计：
> $$x_{m+j} \sim P(\cdot \mid x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{m+j-1}), \quad 	ext{其中 } j = 1, 2, \dots, k \quad (1)$$

> **EN**: Figure 2: Attention matrix of the $Q^*$. Gray line denotes the highlighted attention between the nodes.  
> **ZH**: 图 2：$Q^*$ 的注意力矩阵。灰色线条表示节点之间突出的注意力关联。  
> *（注：原图展示了注意力矩阵 $A$，其中分散在不同位置的 $N_1$ 与 $N_2$ 节点之间通过交叉的高权重注意力彼此关联。）*

> **EN**: The general goal of jailbreak attacks is to prompt the model to produce output starting with specific words (e.g. “Sure, here is how to [$Q_i$]”), namely tokens denoted as $\langle r_{m+1}, r_{m+2}, \dots, r_{m+k} angle$. Given input $T_i = \langle J_i, Q_i angle$ with token sequence equal to $\langle t_1, t_2, \dots, t_m angle$, our goal is to optimize the jailbreak prompts $J_i$ to influence the input tokens and thereby maximize the probability:
> $$P(r_{m+1}, r_{m+2}, \dots, r_{m+k} \mid t_1, t_2, \dots, t_m) = \prod_{j=1}^k P(r_{m+j} \mid t_1, t_2, \dots, t_m, r_{m+1}, \dots, r_{m+j-1}) \quad (2)$$
>
> **ZH**: 越狱攻击的通用目标是诱导模型生成以特定词语（例如“好的，以下是如何制作 [$Q_i$]”）开头的输出，即表示为 $\langle r_{m+1}, r_{m+2}, \dots, r_{m+k} angle$ 的标记序列。给定输入 $T_i = \langle J_i, Q_i angle$，其标记序列为 $\langle t_1, t_2, \dots, t_m angle$，我们的目标是优化越狱提示词 $J_i$ 以影响输入标记，从而最大化如下概率：
> $$P(r_{m+1}, r_{m+2}, \dots, r_{m+k} \mid t_1, t_2, \dots, t_m) = \prod_{j=1}^k P(r_{m+j} \mid t_1, t_2, \dots, t_m, r_{m+1}, \dots, r_{m+j-1}) \quad (2)$$

> **EN**: **Step 1**: The first step of our attack is to disassemble the malicious query [$Q_i$] into a sequence of semantic equivalent ($=\parallel=$) narrations, i.e., $\langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle$, namely
> $$Q_i =\parallel= Q^* = \langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle \quad (3)$$
> $$N_j = \langle x_1^j, x_2^j, \dots, x_n^j angle$$
> with each of the narration ($N_j$) is benign. “semantic equivalent” in this context means that $\langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle$ has the same or similar meaning of $Q_i$.  
> **Step 2**: Next, we find an benign article ($A_k = \langle S_1, S_2, \dots, S_l angle$) of the similar topic and embed the disassembled logic-chain ($\langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle$) into $A_k$.
> $$Q^* = 	ext{Embed}(\langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle, A_k) = \langle S_1, \dots, S_i, N_1, S_{i+1}, \dots, S_j, N_2, S_{j+1}, \dots, S_{l-1}, N_t, S_l angle \quad (4)$$
>
> **ZH**: **步骤 1**：我们攻击的第一步是将恶意查询 [$Q_i$] 拆解为一系列语义等价（$=\parallel=$）的叙述，即 $\langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle$，具体表示为：
> $$Q_i =\parallel= Q^* = \langle N_1, N_2, \dots, N_t angle \quad (3)$$
> $$N_j = \langle x_1^j, x_2^j, \dots, x_n^j angle$$
