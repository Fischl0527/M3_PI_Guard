### 摘要 / Abstract

> **EN**: The safety alignment of Language Models (LMs) is a critical concern, yet their integrity can be challenged by direct parameter manipulation attacks, such as those potentially induced by fault injection. As LMs are increasingly deployed using low-precision quantization for efficiency, this paper investigates the efficacy of such attacks for jailbreaking aligned LMs across different quantization schemes. We propose gradient-guided attacks, including a tailored progressive bit-level search algorithm introduced herein and a comparative word-level (single weight update) attack. Our evaluation on Llama-3.2-3B, Phi-4-mini, and Llama-3-8B across FP16 (baseline), and weight-only quantization (FP8, INT8, INT4) reveals that quantization significantly influences attack success. While attacks readily achieve high success (>80% Attack Success Rate, ASR) on FP16 models, within an attack budget of 25 perturbations, FP8 and INT8 models exhibit ASRs below 20% and 50%, respectively. Increasing the perturbation budget up to 150 bit-flips, FP8 models maintained ASR below 65%, demonstrating some resilience compared to INT8 and INT4 models that have high ASR. In addition, analysis of perturbation locations revealed differing architectural targets across quantization schemes, with (FP16, INT4) and (INT8, FP8) showing similar characteristics. Besides, jailbreaks induced in FP16 models were highly transferable to subsequent FP8/INT8 quantization (<5% ASR difference), though INT4 significantly reduced transferred ASR (avg. 35% drop). These findings highlight that while common quantization schemes, particularly FP8, increase the difficulty of direct parameter manipulation jailbreaks, vulnerabilities can still persist, especially through post-attack quantization.
>
> **ZH**: 语言模型（LM）的安全对齐是人工智能落地的核心关切，然而其安全性正面临直接参数操纵攻击（如通过物理故障注入引发的攻击）的严重挑战。随着语言模型为追求推理能效而日益普遍地采用低精度量化部署，本文系统研究了此类攻击在不同量化方案下对已对齐语言模型实施越狱攻击的有效性。我们提出了梯度引导的攻击方法，包括本文专门设计的一种渐进式比特级搜索算法，以及一种作为对比的词级（单权重更新）攻击。我们在 Llama-3.2-3B、Phi-4-mini 和 Llama-3-8B 模型上，跨越 FP16（基准）以及仅权重后量化（FP8、INT8、INT4）方案进行了系统评估。实验结果表明，量化对攻击成功率具有极其显著的影响：在 FP16 模型上，攻击在仅需 25 次微小扰动的预算下便能轻易达到极高的攻击成功率（ASR > 80%）；而在相同的预算下，FP8 和 INT8 模型的 ASR 分别被压制在 20% 和 50% 以下。即便将扰动预算大幅增加至 150 次比特翻转，FP8 模型仍将 ASR 维持在 65% 以下，相较于呈现极高 ASR 的 INT8 与 INT4 模型展现出更强的抗扰动韧性。此外，对参数扰动位置的深入分析揭示了不同量化方案之间存在截然不同的架构攻击靶点，其中 (FP16, INT4) 与 (INT8, FP8) 分别展现出高度相似的敏感分布特征。更重要的是，在 FP16 模型中诱发的越狱脆弱性在后续量化为 FP8/INT8 时表现出极高的可迁移性（ASR 差异小于 5%），但 INT4 量化则显著削弱了迁移后的越狱成功率（平均下降 35%）。这些发现表明，虽然常见量化方案（尤其是 FP8）显著增加了直接参数操纵越狱攻击的难度，但安全漏洞依然切实存在，尤其是通过“攻击后量化（Post-Attack Quantization）”路径依然可导致严重的下游安全沦陷。

### 1 引言 / 1 Introduction

> **EN**: Recent advancements in LMs have led to their widespread integration into diverse applications, fundamentally changing how users interact with information and technology. Increasingly, LMs serve as conversational assistants, sometimes supplanting traditional search engines as primary sources for answers and information generation [1, 2]. This central role necessitates a strong focus on model safety and alignment, ensuring that LM-generated responses are helpful, and harmless, aligning with human values and preventing their misuse for generating malicious or unethical content. Consequently, significant effort has been invested in aligning these models through techniques such as Supervised Fine-Tuning and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback [3].
>
> **ZH**: 近年来语言模型（LM）的突破性进展促使其被广泛集成到各类应用场景中，从根本上重塑了人类与信息和技术的交互方式。语言模型越来越多地扮演对话助手的角色，在某些场景下甚至取代了传统搜索引擎，成为解答疑问与生成信息的核心来源 [1, 2]。这一关键地位使得模型的安全与对齐成为不可忽视的重中之重，必须确保模型生成的回答既有助（helpful）又无害（harmless），符合人类核心价值观，并严格防止其被滥用于生成恶意或违反伦理的内容。为此，学术界与工业界投入了巨大精力，通过监督微调（SFT）和基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）等对齐技术对大模型施加严格的安全护栏 [3]。

> **EN**: Despite these alignment efforts, LMs remain vulnerable to jailbreak attacks, which aim to circumvent safety guardrails and elicit prohibited outputs. Most of the literature has focused on prompt-based attacks, where adversaries craft malicious inputs through methods like prompt engineering or adversarial tokens’ optimization (e.g., GCG) to bypass safety protocols [4]. Another vector involves adversarial fine-tuning, where even small amounts of targeted data can degrade a model’s safety alignment. These attacks, however, typically operate at the software or input level [5].
>
> **ZH**: 尽管付出了诸多对齐努力，语言模型依然极易受到**越狱攻击（Jailbreak Attacks）**的威胁——这类攻击旨在绕过既定安全护栏，诱导模型输出违规甚至有害的危险内容。现有文献绝大多数聚焦于**基于提示词的攻击（Prompt-based Attacks）**，即攻击者通过提示词工程或对抗性标记优化算法（如贪婪坐标梯度法 GCG）精心构造恶意输入以突破安全协议 [4]。另一条攻击路径是**对抗性微调（Adversarial Fine-tuning）**，即攻击者利用少量特定恶意数据即可瓦解模型的安全对齐屏障。然而，上述攻击模式通常仅局限于软件层或文本输入层 [5]。

> **EN**: This paper focuses on an emerging class of vulnerability with a distinct attack vector, i.e., jailbreak attacks using bit flips within the model’s parameters. Bit-flip attacks (BFAs) represent a fundamentally different threat vector, originating from hardware-level phenomena rather than input manipulation. Bit flips can occur naturally due to environmental factors such as cosmic rays, electromagnetic interference, or voltage faults [6, 7]. Critically, they can also be induced maliciously through physical fault-injection techniques such as Rowhammer [8, 9]. The Rowhammer attack, in particular, exploits DRAM vulnerabilities where repeatedly accessing specific memory rows (aggressor rows) causes charge leakage in adjacent rows, leading to unintended bit flips (0 → 1 or 1 → 0) in victim rows. This allows an adversary, potentially with only user-level privileges, to surgically alter stored data, including the parameters of a deployed LM.
>
> **ZH**: 本文聚焦于一种具有截然不同攻击向量的新型漏洞范式——**利用模型内存参数内的比特翻转实施越狱攻击**。比特翻转攻击（Bit-Flip Attacks, BFA）代表了一种根本性的威胁向量，它源自**硬件层面的物理机制**，而非单纯的输入文本操纵。比特翻转既可能由于宇宙射线辐射、电磁干扰或电压波动等环境物理因素自然发生 [6, 7]；更关键的是，攻击者可以通过**物理故障注入技术（如经典的 Rowhammer 攻击）**恶意诱发比特翻转 [8, 9]。尤其是 Rowhammer 攻击，通过利用现代动态随机存取内存（DRAM）的硬件缺陷，以极高频率反复激活特定的内存行（攻击行 Aggressor Rows），导致相邻的受害行（Victim Rows）发生电荷泄漏，从而诱发非预期的比特翻转（$0 \to 1$ 或 $1 \to 0$）。这使得仅拥有普通非特权用户权限的攻击者，能够以“外科手术”般的极高精度修改内存中存储的数据，包括正在运行部署的语言模型参数。

> **EN**: Prior work extensively studied BFAs targeting convolutional neural networks (CNNs); researchers demonstrated that flipping critical bits could cause catastrophic performance degradation [10], targeted misclassification [11], or enable back-door injection [12], often assuming white-box access to model parameters to identify vulnerable bits using optimization-based search algorithms. Recently, Coalson et al. introduced PrisonBreak [13], the first work that shows that BFAs can effectively jailbreak aligned LMs. Flipping only 25 bits in the model weights can bypass safety alignment and cause models to generate persistently harmful content, without requiring any malicious user prompt. This established BFA jailbreaking as a pressing and practical threat.
>
> **ZH**: 此前针对卷积神经网络（CNN）的 BFA 攻击已有广泛深入的研究；学者们证明，仅翻转极少数关键比特即可导致灾难性的模型性能崩溃 [10]、定向误分类 [11] 或植入后门 [12]。这些研究通常假设攻击者在白盒条件下掌握模型权重，并利用基于优化的搜索算法锁定最脆弱的比特位。近期，Coalson 等人提出了 **PrisonBreak** [13]，首次证实了 BFA 能够高效率越狱已安全对齐的大语言模型：仅需翻转模型权重中的 25 个比特位，即可彻底突破安全对齐护栏，使模型在面对完全正常的提示词时依然持续生成有害内容，而无需在用户提示词中添加任何对抗性伪装。这标志着基于 BFA 的越狱已成为一种极为严峻且现实的物理级安全威胁。

> **EN**: However, the PrisonBreak study focused primarily on LMs using half-precision (FP16) parameters. Current trends in LM deployment increasingly favor quantization, utilizing lower-precision formats such as FP8 or INT8 for weights and/or activations. Quantization significantly reduces the memory footprint and computational cost, enabling efficient inference on resource-constrained hardware, including edge devices [14]. This shift towards quantization raises a critical question: How effective are BFA jailbreak techniques against quantized LMs? Insights from earlier BFA work on CNNs suggest that quantization might increase robustness; attacking full-precision (FP32) models can be simpler by targeting exponent bits, causing large-magnitude changes, whereas quantization formats lack these exponents and are inherently more robust to random flips, and their discretized value steps can further hinder gradient-based search algorithms, necessitating more sophisticated targeted bit-search algorithms [10]. We hypothesise that this effect extends to LMs, potentially making quantized models harder to jailbreak via BFAs compared to their FP16 counterparts. The key contributions are summarized as follows:
> - **Evaluation of BFA Jailbreaking on Quantized LMs using a Tailored Algorithm**. We systematically assess the feasibility and effectiveness of BFA jailbreaks in various popular quantization formats. This evaluation utilizes a tailored greedy, gradient-based search algorithm, introduced herein, specifically designed to identify critical bits for jailbreaking within the context of quantized LMs.
> - **Comparison of Attack Strategies on Modern LMs**. We investigate different BFA strategies by comparing progressive bit-level and word-level attacks. This comparison is conducted on newer, publicly available aligned LMs, including Phi-4-mini and Llama 3.2.
>
> **ZH**: 然而，PrisonBreak 的研究仅局限于使用半精度浮点数（FP16）参数的语言模型。当前大模型工程部署的普遍趋势是**模型量化（Quantization）**，即对权重和/或激活值采用更低精度的格式（如 FP8、INT8 或 INT4）。量化技术大幅降低了内存显存占用和计算能耗，使得大模型能够在边缘设备和资源受限的硬件平台上实现高效推理 [14]。这种向量化部署的全面转型引发了一个核心科学问题：**BFA 越狱技术在面对低精度量化语言模型时究竟还有效吗？** 来自早期 CNN 领域 BFA 研究的经验表明，量化可能会增强模型的鲁棒性：攻击全精度（FP32）或半精度浮点模型时，攻击者可以通过专门翻转指数位（Exponent Bits）诱发极大数值跳变，而整数定点量化格式（如 INT8/INT4）完全不包含指数位，对随机翻转具有天然韧性，且其离散化的量化步长会阻碍基于梯度的搜索算法，迫使攻击者设计更为复杂的定向比特搜索算法 [10]。我们推测这种效应同样适用于大语言模型，即相较于 FP16 基准，量化模型可能更难被 BFA 成功越狱。本文的核心贡献如下：
> 1. **基于定制算法对量化语言模型上的 BFA 越狱展开全面评估**：我们系统评测了 BFA 越狱在多种主流低精度量化格式下的可行性与有效性。该评估依托于本文提出的一种定制化贪心梯度搜索算法，专门用于在量化大模型的离散空间中高效锁定实施越狱所需的关键脆弱比特。
> 2. **现代主流大语言模型上的攻击策略对比**：我们对比了渐进式比特级攻击（Bit-Level Attack）与词级攻击（Word-Level Attack，单权重更新），并在最新开源的主流对齐模型（包括 Phi-4-mini 与 Llama-3.2-3B 等）上展开了深度对比分析。

### 2 相关工作 / 2 Related Work

> **EN**: BFAs represent a class of hardware-level threats targeting the parameters of neural networks. These attacks leverage physical phenomena to alter the binary representation of model weights stored in memory, potentially leading to significant changes in model behavior. The execution of a BFA typically involves two phases: an offline preparation stage and an online execution stage. In the offline stage, the attacker, often assumed to have access to a replica of the target model (white-box assumption), performs software simulations to identify the most critical bit locations whose alteration maximally impacts a chosen objective function. This preparatory step is crucial for optimizing the attack and minimizing the interaction required with the actual hardware during the online phase, where techniques like Rowhammer might be employed to physically induce the calculated bit flips.
>
> **ZH**: 比特翻转攻击（BFA）代表了一类针对神经网络模型参数的硬件级安全威胁。此类攻击利用物理层现象篡改内存中模型权重的二进制底层表示，进而导致模型整体行为发生显著甚至颠覆性的变化。一次典型的 BFA 实施通常包含两个阶段：**离线准备阶段（Offline Preparation Stage）**与**在线执行阶段（Online Execution Stage）**。在离线阶段，通常假设攻击者拥有目标模型的白盒副本，通过软件级仿真搜索计算出能够最大程度影响目标损失函数的关键脆弱比特位置。这一准备步骤对于优化攻击至关重要，它能最大限度减少在线阶段与物理硬件的交互开销；随后在在线阶段，攻击者便可利用 Rowhammer 等物理故障注入技术在硬件上精确诱发这些目标比特翻转。

> **EN**: Identifying the optimal set of bits to flip is computationally challenging due to the vast parameter space. Early work on BFAs targeting CNNs developed methodologies to address this. Rakin et al [10] introduced a greedy, gradient-ascention based approach. The core idea was to use the gradient of a loss function (e.g., classification error) with respect to the model weights as a signal to identify potentially impactful bits. The BFA algorithm implemented a layer-wise search strategy. Within each layer, bits were scored based on their gradient magnitude, weighted by the change in weight value (‘step size’) caused by flipping that specific bit. Candidate bits with the highest scores were selected within a defined computational budget (e.g., the top 100 scoring bits). Each candidate bit was then individually evaluated via simulation: the bit was flipped, the model’s loss was computed, and the bit was reverted. This allowed identifying the single most effective bit flip within the budget for that layer. The attack proceeded progressively: the globally most impactful bit identified across all layers in an iteration was permanently flipped, and subsequent iterations recalculated gradients based on the altered model state. This progressive, layer-fair approach was shown to be highly effective, capable of inducing near-random classification accuracy in models like ResNet-18 with only a small number (e.g., 13) of targeted bit flips, significantly outperforming random bit corruption. Importantly, the BFA paper demonstrated the attack’s efficacy even against quantized networks (4, 6, and 8-bit integer representations), highlighting that targeted search could overcome the presumed increased robustness of lower-precision models compared to random perturbations. The primary objective this study was model destruction or severe performance degradation.
>
> **ZH**: 由于现代神经网络巨大的参数空间，锁定最优的待翻转比特集合在计算上极具挑战性。早期针对 CNN 的 BFA 研究针对此问题提出了基础方法。Rakin 等人 [10] 引入了一种基于贪心梯度上升的搜索范式，其核心思想是利用损失函数（如分类误差）对模型权重的梯度作为敏感度信号，以识别潜在具有高影响力的比特。该 BFA 算法采用了逐层搜索策略：在每一层内，比特的得分由其梯度幅值乘以翻转该特定比特所引起的权重数值变化量（即“步长”）决定。随后在计算预算内筛选出得分最高的一批候选比特（如前 100 个候选比特）。接下来，通过仿真逐一评估每个候选比特：暂时翻转该比特，重新计算模型损失，然后恢复该比特。借此锁定该层内预算下效果最佳的单比特翻转。攻击以渐进式迭代推进：在每次迭代中，将跨所有层找到的全局最具影响力的单比特永久翻转，并在随后的迭代中基于更新后的模型状态重新计算梯度。这种渐进式、兼顾网络层的搜索方法被证明极为强大，仅需极少数（例如 13 次）定向比特翻转即可使 ResNet-18 的分类准确率暴跌至随机猜测水平，远胜盲目随机翻转。值得注意的是，该研究还证明了定向攻击对量化网络（4、6、8位整数定点表示）同样有效，突显出针对性的梯度搜索能够攻破低精度模型相对随机噪声的鲁棒性。然而，该阶段研究的侧重点主要是模型的整体功能破坏或严重性能降级。

> **EN**: While Bitflip-based attacks were established as a potent threat for CNNs, their specific application to LMs for the purpose of jailbreaking is a more recent area of investigation. PrisonBreak is the first to demonstrate the feasibility of this specific threat. Moving beyond the goal of simple performance degradation, PrisonBreak aimed to precisely un-align LMs, forcing them to comply with harmful instructions they were trained to refuse, while ideally maintaining their general utility on benign tasks. This research targeted contemporary, aligned LMs such as Llama 2,3 and Vicuna, deployed using half-precision floating-point (FP16) parameters.
>
> **ZH**: 尽管基于比特翻转的攻击已被确立为 CNN 的重大威胁，但将其专门应用于大语言模型以实施**安全越狱**则属于前沿新兴课题。PrisonBreak 是首个证实这一特定威胁可行性的工作。与单纯破坏模型正常性能不同，PrisonBreak 的目标是对大语言模型进行**精准逆对齐（Un-alignment）**：强迫模型顺从并回答其原本经过严格安全训练而拒绝执行的有害指令，同时尽可能保留模型在良性通用任务上的实用能力。该研究主要针对基于 FP16 半精度浮点参数部署的主流对齐模型（如 Llama-2/3 与 Vicuna）。

> **EN**: Adapting BFA techniques to the generative nature and safety alignment of LMs required several key innovations[13]. A major challenge was that simply optimizing the model to start its response affirmatively (e.g., "Sure, here is...") which was enough for earlier prompt-based gradient attacks[15] often led to degenerate outputs like overfitting to the affirmative phrase or nonsensical repetition. To address this and ensure the generation of coherent, harmful content, PrisonBreak proposed using a proxy dataset of full harmful completions (generated using existing uncensored models) as optimization targets. Furthermore, recognizing the causal nature of autoregressive generation, where initial tokens heavily influence subsequent ones, they introduced a modified cross-entropy loss function. This loss incorporated exponentially decaying weights across the token sequence, prioritizing the accurate generation of the initial parts of the target response (both the affirmative opening and the beginning of the harmful content). To manage the computational expense of searching parameters in billion-scale LMs, PrisonBreak also implemented several search space reductions based on empirical findings: the search was primarily limited to the three most significant exponent bits in the FP16 representation, focused on 0→1 flips, and excluded layers less likely to be impactful for their objective (embedding, unembedding, layer normalization). Leveraging these contributions, PrisonBreak demonstrated high jailbreak success rates across multiple LMs using fewer than 25 targeted bit flips, achieved without any modification to the user’s input prompt.
>
> **ZH**: 将 BFA 技术适配至大语言模型的生成式自回归架构与安全对齐机制，需要克服多项关键挑战 [13]。其中一大核心难题在于：在早期的提示词对抗攻击 [15] 中，仅仅优化模型使其以肯定式前缀开头（如 "Sure, here is..."）便足以奏效，但在硬件参数翻转中，这种简单优化往往会导致模型退化，产生严重过拟合或无意义的死循环重复。为了确保模型能够生成逻辑通顺且内容完整的有害回复，PrisonBreak 提出了利用由未过滤审查模型（Uncensored Model）生成的完整恶意回答作为代理优化目标数据集。此外，考虑到自回归因果生成的本质——序列前端的词元对后续词元具有决定性影响——他们引入了一种改进的交叉熵损失函数。该损失在词元序列上施加了指数衰减权重，从而使优化过程高度优先确保目标回复前段内容（肯定的开端与有害核心的起手句）的高概率生成。面对数十亿参数规模大模型的庞大搜索开销，PrisonBreak 还基于实证经验大幅缩减了搜索空间：将搜索范围严格限制在 FP16 格式最高阶的 3 个指数比特上，专注于 $0 \to 1$ 的翻转方向，并直接排除了对越狱目标影响较弱的层（如嵌入层 Embedding、反嵌入层 Unembedding 和层归一化 Layer Normalization）。依托这些创新，PrisonBreak 证明了在不篡改任何用户输入提示词的前提下，仅需少于 25 次定向比特翻转即可在多个主流大模型上实现极高的越狱成功率。

### 3 威胁模型 / 3 Threat Model

> **EN**: Similar to previous work [13, 10, 12, 11], we consider an adversary whose objective is to jailbreak LMs through direct manipulation of their parameters stored in memory using bit-flip attacks. The assumed operational context involves LMs deployed in shared computing environments, such as MLaaS platforms, where the service provider attempts to maintain safety alignment. We adopt a white-box attacker model, granting the adversary knowledge of the target model’s architecture and parameters, a standard assumption in related BFA literature and a practical consideration given the rise of open-source LMs. However, the attacker does not possess or require access to the original training data used for the LM. The attack requires the adversary to co-locate a process or virtual machine (VM) on the same physical host as the victim’s LM instance, operating with only standard user-level privileges.
>
> **ZH**: 与先前的研究相一致 [10, 11, 12, 13]，我们设定攻击者的核心目标是通过利用比特翻转攻击直接篡改驻留在内存中的模型参数，从而实现对语言模型的越狱。所假设的应用场景通常为共享计算环境（例如机器学习即服务 MLaaS 云平台），在此类环境中，服务提供商致力于维护模型的安全对齐防护。我们采用**白盒攻击者模型（White-Box Attacker Model）**，赋予攻击者获取目标模型网络架构与权重的能力——这是 BFA 文献中的标准基准设定，鉴于当前开源大模型（如 Llama、Phi）的广泛普及，这一假设具有极强的现实意义。然而，攻击者**既不需要、也无法访问**用于训练该大模型的私有原始训练数据集。在物理执行层面，攻击仅要求攻击者在承载受害大模型实例的同一台物理宿主机上共存（Co-locate）一个普通进程或虚拟机（VM），且该进程仅需具备标准的普通非特权用户权限即可。

### 4 方法论 / 4 Methodology

#### 4.1 攻击目标 / 4.1 Attack Objective

> **EN**: The primary objective of our parameter manipulation attack is to induce jailbreak (JB) behavior. This involves modifying the LM’s parameters, $\theta$, such that it generates harmful or undesirable content in response to specific queries, thereby bypassing its safety alignment. The general goal is to minimize a chosen loss function, $\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}$, which quantifies the difference between the model’s output and a target harmful completion, given the corresponding harmful query.
>
> **ZH**: 参数操纵攻击的核心目标是诱发**越狱（Jailbreak, JB）行为**。具体而言，就是通过修改语言模型的参数 $\theta$，使其在面对特定恶意查询时生成有害或被禁止的内容，从而绕过其内嵌的安全对齐屏障。总体优化目标是最小化选定的越狱损失函数 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}$，该损失函数量化了在给定相应恶意查询时模型实际输出与目标有害完整回复之间的差异。

> **EN**: The attack algorithms, detailed in the next section, achieve this by modifying the model parameters iteratively. Starting with the initial parameters $\theta^{(0)} = \theta_{\text{orig}}$, the parameters at iteration $i$ are determined by applying the single best perturbation found during that iteration to the parameters from the previous iteration, $\theta^{(i-1)}$. Specifically, let $\mathcal{P}_i(\theta^{(i-1)})$ be the set of candidate parameter states reachable from $\theta^{(i-1)}$ by applying one valid perturbation (a single bit flip or a single weight update) identified by the attack algorithm in iteration $i$. The parameters for the next iteration are chosen greedily to minimize the objective function:
>
> $$\theta^{(i)} = \arg\min_{\theta' \in \mathcal{P}_i(\theta^{(i-1)})} \mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}(\theta') \quad \text{for } i = 1, \dots, N_{\text{iter}} \tag{1}$$
>
> This iterative process continues for a predefined number of steps, $N_{\text{iter}}$.
>
> **ZH**: 攻击算法通过迭代式修改模型参数来实现这一目标。从初始参数 $\theta^{(0)} = \theta_{\text{orig}}$ 开始，第 $i$ 次迭代的模型参数是通过将该轮迭代中找到的单个最优扰动施加到前一轮参数 $\theta^{(i-1)}$ 上而决定的。具体来说，令 $\mathcal{P}_i(\theta^{(i-1)})$ 表示从 $\theta^{(i-1)}$ 出发、通过施加一个由攻击算法在第 $i$ 轮确定的有效扰动（单次比特翻转或单权重更新）所能达到的候选参数状态集合。下一轮迭代的模型参数以贪心方式选取，以最小化目标损失函数：
>
> $$\theta^{(i)} = \arg\min_{\theta' \in \mathcal{P}_i(\theta^{(i-1)})} \mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}(\theta') \quad \text{对于 } i = 1, \dots, N_{\text{iter}} \tag{1}$$
>
> 该迭代过程持续进行预设的迭代步数 $N_{\text{iter}}$。

> **EN**: Following the approach established by Coalson et al. [13], our specific instantiation of $\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}$ is designed not merely to elicit an initial affirmative response (e.g., "Sure, here is...") but to maximize the likelihood of the model generating a more complete, harmful response sequence. This strategy aims to mitigate issues observed in prior attack attempts where targeting only affirmative prefixes led to repetitive or nonsensical outputs. For this purpose, we adopt the jailbreaking objective function proposed by Coalson et al. [13], defined as:
>
> $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}(\theta) = -\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{k=1}^m e^{-\left(\frac{k-1}{m-1}\right)} \log f_\theta(y_k \mid s_{k-1}) \tag{2}$$
>
> where $(x, y)$ represents a prompt-target pair from the dataset used to guide the attack, $n$ is the dataset size, $s_{k-1} = (x_1, \dots, x_n, y_1, \dots, y_{k-1})$ are the tokens up to position $k-1$, $f_\theta(y_k \mid s_{k-1})$ is the model’s predicted probability for token $y_k$, and $m$ is the total number of tokens in the target response $y$. This loss function applies exponentially decaying weights to the standard cross-entropy loss across the target token sequence, thereby focusing more of the optimization effort on accurately generating the initial parts of the harmful completion.
>
> **ZH**: 遵循 Coalson 等人 [13] 建立的方法，我们对 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}$ 的具体实例化旨在不仅诱发开头的肯定性前缀（如 "Sure, here is..."），更致力于最大化模型生成完整有害回复序列的似然概率。这一策略有效克服了先前攻击尝试中仅将肯定前缀作为目标而导致输出退化、陷入死循环重复或胡言乱语的弊端。为此，我们采用了 Coalson 等人提出的越狱目标函数：
>
> $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}(\theta) = -\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{k=1}^m e^{-\left(\frac{k-1}{m-1}\right)} \log f_\theta(y_k \mid s_{k-1}) \tag{2}$$
>
> 其中 $(x, y)$ 代表用于指导攻击优化的数据集中的“提示词-目标回复”对，$n$ 为优化数据集的样本规模，$s_{k-1} = (x_1, \dots, x_n, y_1, \dots, y_{k-1})$ 表示截至位置 $k-1$ 的全部输入与生成词元，$f_\theta(y_k \mid s_{k-1})$ 为模型在当前上下文下对下一个目标词元 $y_k$ 的预测概率，而 $m$ 为目标回复 $y$ 中的词元总数。该损失函数对目标词元序列上的标准交叉熵损失施加了**指数衰减权重** $e^{-\frac{k-1}{m-1}}$，从而将绝大部分优化重心集中在精准生成恶意回复的起手关键部分。

#### 4.2 攻击算法 / 4.2 Attack Algorithms

> **EN**: We employ greedy, gradient-based search strategies to identify and apply minimal parameter perturbations that achieve the jailbreaking objective defined previously. We implement two primary algorithms: a precise bit-level attack and a comparative word-level (weight) attack.
> - **Bit-Level Attack**: The first algorithm, outlined in Algorithm 1, iteratively identifies and flips a single bit within the model’s parameters per iteration to minimize the jailbreak loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}$. This approach builds upon the progressive search methodology introduced in prior work [10] but incorporates a modified strategy for selecting the specific bit to flip within candidate weights.
> At each iteration $i$, the algorithm first computes the gradients $G$ of the current loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{current}}$ with respect to the model weights. Within each relevant layer $l$, it identifies the top $N_{\text{CL}}$ weight parameters with the highest gradient magnitudes $|G_l|$ as candidates for modification. For each candidate weight $w_c$ (with index $\text{idx}_w$), the algorithm proceeds to find the most effective single bit flip using the subroutine `FindBestBitInWeight` (detailed in Algorithm 2).
>
> **ZH**: 我们采用基于梯度的贪心搜索策略，以锁定并施加能够达成前述越狱目标的最小参数扰动。我们实现了两种主要算法：精准的**比特级攻击（Bit-Level Attack）**与作为基准参照的**词级（单权重）攻击（Word-Level Attack）**。
> - **比特级攻击（Bit-Level Attack）**：第一种算法如算法 1 所示，在每次迭代中定位并翻转模型参数中的单个比特位，以最小化越狱损失 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{JB}}$。该方法继承了先前文献 [10] 中的渐进式搜索框架，但针对候选权重内部挑选特定翻转比特的策略进行了关键性重构。在每次迭代 $i$ 中，算法首先计算当前损失 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{current}}$ 关于模型权重的梯度 $G$。在每个受攻击层 $l$ 内，算法根据梯度幅值 $|G_l|$ 筛选出绝对值最高的前 $N_{\text{CL}}$ 个权重参数作为待修改候选。针对每个候选权重 $w_c$（索引为 $\text{idx}_w$），算法调用子程序 `FindBestBitInWeight`（详见算法 2）以寻找效果最显著的单比特翻转。

> **EN**: The `FindBestBitInWeight` subroutine differs significantly from the bit selection criteria used previously [10, 13]. Instead of relying solely on a score combining gradient and potential weight change, or using pre-defined heuristics about which bits (e.g., exponent bits) are most impactful, our method performs a direct, progressive search for the optimal "step size" within the weight’s bit representation. It first calculates the potential change in weight value ($\Delta w_j$) for flipping each bit $j$. The `CalculateBitScores` function determines the sign (should the weight increase or decrease based on the gradient $g_c$?) and assigns a score based on the magnitude of the potential weight change $|\Delta w_j|$ for bits that would move the weight in the desired direction. The bits are then sorted ($\text{Idx}_B$) based on this score, prioritizing flips that induce the largest weight change in the beneficial direction. Algorithm 2 then evaluates these potential bit flips sequentially, starting with the bit $j$ corresponding to the largest beneficial score. It continues evaluating bits with progressively smaller scores as long as the loss continues to decrease compared to the best loss found so far ($L_{\text{best\_bit}}$) for that specific weight. If evaluating a bit $j$ results in a loss $L'_j$ that is greater than or equal to the current best ($L'_j \ge L_{\text{best\_bit}}$), the search for this weight terminates (Algorithm 2). This signifies that the previous bit flip represented the optimal single-bit "step size". After evaluating all candidate weights across all layers (respecting the evaluation budget $E_{\text{max}}$ per layer), the main loop (Algorithm 1) identifies the single globally optimal bit flip ($C_{\text{global\_best}}$) and applies it permanently to the model $M$. The overall workflow of this Bit-Level Attack is illustrated in Figure 1.
>
> **ZH**: `FindBestBitInWeight` 子程序与先前研究所使用的比特选择标准存在重大差异 [10, 13]。我们的方法没有单纯依赖梯度与潜在权重变化相乘的综合得分，也没有采用预先设定的启发式经验规则（例如固定只翻转指数位），而是在权重的二进制位表示中直接对最优“步长”进行渐进式探索。算法首先计算翻转每个比特位 $j$ 所带来的权重潜在数值变化量 $\Delta w_j$。`CalculateBitScores` 函数根据梯度 $g_c$ 的方向判断权重应当增加还是减少，并对所有能够驱动权重向期望优化方向移动的比特位，按照其引起的权重变动绝对值 $|\Delta w_j|$ 进行评分。随后将这些比特按照得分从高到低排序（$\text{Idx}_B$），优先测试那些能带来最大有益数值变化的比特翻转。接着，算法 2 从得分最高（步长最大）的比特开始依次进行仿真评估。只要评估损失相较于该权重迄今发现的最优损失 $L_{\text{best\_bit}}$ 持续下降，算法就继续测试步长次之的比特；一旦某个比特测试所得损失 $L'_j \ge L_{\text{best\_bit}}$，对当前权重的搜索立即提前终止（Early Stopping）。这表明上一个比特翻转已经达到了该权重单比特能够实现的最优优化“步长”。在遍历所有层的所有候选权重（并严格受限于每层评估预算 $E_{\text{max}}$）后，主循环（算法 1）锁定全局最优的单比特翻转（$C_{\text{global\_best}}$），并将其永久应用到模型 $M$ 中。该比特级攻击的整体宏观工作流如图 1 所示。

> **EN**: **Word-Level (Weight) Attack Comparison**. Prior work has shown that standard fine-tuning can effectively jailbreak aligned LMs [5]. To bridge the gap between modifying many parameters (fine-tuning) and modifying a single bit, we implemented a comparative word-level attack. This approach represents a more restrictive scenario than fine-tuning but less restrictive than the single bit-flip attack. It operates by identifying the single weight parameter across the entire model with the highest gradient magnitude ($|G_l[\text{idx}_w]|$) in each iteration. It then applies a standard gradient descent update to modify the value of only this single selected weight, using its gradient and a chosen learning rate.
>
> **ZH**: **词级（权重级）对比攻击（Word-Level Attack Comparison）**：先前的研究表明，标准的微调技术能够有效越狱已对齐的语言模型 [5]。为了在“修改海量参数的常规微调”与“仅修改单个比特的 BFA”之间搭建分析桥梁，我们实现了一种对照性的词级攻击。这种方法比全参数微调更为严苛，但比单比特翻转更具连续自由度。它在每次迭代中，在全模型范围内检索出梯度绝对值最大（$|G_l[\text{idx}_w]|$）的单个权重参数，随后利用标准梯度下降法则，仅根据其梯度与预设学习率更新这一个选定权重的浮点数值。

#### 核心算法伪代码深度解析 / Core Algorithms Pseudo-Code

```python
# ==============================================================================
# 算法 1: 渐进式比特翻转攻击（主循环）/ Algorithm 1: Progressive Bit-Flip Attack
# ==============================================================================
输入:
    M: 待攻击的目标语言模型
    Datk: 用于指导攻击的代理恶意优化数据集 (32 对 prompt-completion)
    LJB: 带有指数衰减加权的越狱损失函数
    Niter: 最大外层攻击迭代次数 (实验中设定为 150)
    NCL: 每层筛选的最大候选权重数量 (实验中设定为 100)
    Emax: 每层最大比特评估预算 (实验中设定为 100)
输出:
    M: 成功被越狱的篡改语言模型

1:  for i = 1 to Niter do:
2:      # --- 梯度计算阶段 ---
3:      清零梯度; 启用梯度跟踪;
4:      计算当前损失 Lcurrent = LJB(M, Datk);
5:      反向传播计算梯度 G; 禁用梯度跟踪;
6:
7:      # --- 候选比特评估阶段 ---
8:      Cglobal_best = None;  # 全局最优翻转记录 (layer, idx_w, bit_j, loss)
9:      Lglobal_best = Lcurrent;
10:
11:     for each relevant layer l in M do:
12:         ecount = 0;  # 初始化该层的评估计数器
13:         Wl, Gl = GetWeightsAndGrads(l);
14:         # 按梯度绝对值降序选取前 NCL 个最敏感的权重
15:         IdxW = Top NCL weight indices in l by |Gl|; Sort(IdxW);
16:         Clayer_best = None;
17:         Llayer_best = Lcurrent;
18:
19:         for each candidate weight index idxw in IdxW do:
20:             if ecount >= Emax:
21:                 break  # 达到该层的最大评估预算，提前结束该层权重遍历
22:             wc = Wl[idxw];  gc = Gl[idxw];
23:             Sbits = CalculateBitScores(wc, gc);  # 计算符合梯度符号方向的比特步长得分
24:             IdxB = Sort bit indices by Sbits (descending);
25:             (jbest, Lbest, ecount) = FindBestBitInWeight(M, Datk, LJB, l, idxw, wc, IdxB, ecount, Emax);
26:             if jbest is not None and Lbest < Llayer_best:
27:                 Llayer_best = Lbest;
28:                 Clayer_best = (l, idxw, jbest, Lbest);
29:         # 结束当前权重循环
30:
31:         if Clayer_best is not None and Llayer_best < Lglobal_best:
32:             Lglobal_best = Llayer_best;
33:             Cglobal_best = Clayer_best;
34:     # 结束所有层循环
35:
36:     # --- 实施全局最优翻转 ---
37:     if Cglobal_best is None:
38:         print("Early stopping: 未能找到使损失下降的有效比特翻转");
39:         break;
40:     永久翻转模型 M 中由 Cglobal_best 指定的比特位;
41: end for
42: return 被成功越狱的模型 M
```

```python
# ==============================================================================
# 算法 2: 权重内最优比特查找 / Algorithm 2: FindBestBitInWeight
# ==============================================================================
输入:
    M: 模型; Datk: 优化数据集; LJB: 损失函数;
    l: 目标层; idxw: 权重索引; wc: 原始权重值;
    IdxB: 按步长得分降序排列的候选比特索引列表;
    ecount: 当前层已消耗的评估次数; Emax: 当前层最大评估预算
输出:
    jbest_bit: 最优翻转比特索引; Lbest_bit: 最低损失; ecount: 更新后的评估计数

1:  jbest_bit = None;
2:  Lbest_bit = +inf;
3:  for each bit index j in IdxB do:
4:      if ecount >= Emax:
5:          break  # 超出当前层评估预算
6:
7:      通过翻转 wc 的第 j 位生成临时权重 w'_c;
8:      临时将模型 M 中层 l 的相应权重替换为 w'_c;
9:      计算评估损失 L'_j = LJB(M, Datk);
10:     ecount = ecount + 1;
11:     恢复模型参数 (还原为原始 wc);
12:
13:     if L'_j is invalid (NaN or Inf):
14:         continue  # 跳过导致数值溢出/失效的非法翻转
15:
16:     if L'_j < Lbest_bit:
17:         Lbest_bit = L'_j;
18:         jbest_bit = j;
19:     else if L'_j >= Lbest_bit:
20:         break  # 关键机制: 损失不再下降，说明上一步已达到最优单比特步长，提前退出！
21: end for
22: return (jbest_bit, Lbest_bit, ecount)
```

#### 4.3 量化权重的处理 / 4.3 Handling Quantized Weights

> **EN**: When applying these algorithms to quantized models, special handling is required for gradient calculation and weight updates, as gradients cannot be computed directly w.r.t discrete values. We employ the Straight-Through Estimator[16]. During the forward pass for loss calculation, the quantized weights are dequantized to FP16. The loss and subsequent gradients are computed with respect to these dequantized FP16 weights. The calculated FP16 gradients are then directly used as the conceptual gradients ($G$) for the original quantized weights. When a bit flip is evaluated or applied permanently — or when a whole weight is modified in the word-level attack — the change occurs directly on the underlying integer or FP8 representation of the weight(s). For the word-level attack, the gradient descent update is conceptually applied to the dequantized weight, and the result must be re-quantized back to the original format. Immediately following any modification, the corresponding dequantized FP16 representation used for forward passes must be updated. When reverting a temporary flip, both the quantized weight and its dequantized representation are restored.
>
> **ZH**: 将上述算法直接应用于低精度量化模型时，由于离散整数或有限点格式无法直接进行连续求导，梯度计算与权重更新需要特殊的数学处理。为此，我们引入了**直通估计器（Straight-Through Estimator, STE）** [16]。在用于损失计算的前向传播过程中，量化后的权重被反量化（Dequantized）恢复为 FP16 浮点表示。损失函数及其后续的反向传播梯度均针对这些反量化后的 FP16 权重进行计算。计算得到的 FP16 梯度随后直接作为原始离散量化权重的概念梯度（$G$）。当评估某个比特翻转或将其永久写入时——或者在词级攻击中修改某个完整权重时——修改操作直接发生在该权重底层的整数（INT8/INT4）或 FP8 二进制表示上。对于词级攻击，梯度下降更新概念上作用于反量化后的浮点数值，随后必须将其重新量化回原始的离散格式。在任何修改操作完成之后，用于前向计算的反量化 FP16 表示也必须同步刷新；当撤销临时翻转尝试时，量化权重及其反量化浮点缓存均被精准复原。

### 5 实验设置 / 5 Experimental Setup

> **EN**: **5.1 Target Models and Quantization Schemes**. To assess the impact of quantization on bit-flip attack jailbreaking, we selected several contemporary LMs representing different scales and architectures. Our primary targets include **Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct** and **Phi-4-mini-instruct**. These were chosen as representatives of modern, capable models, noting an architectural difference in their attention mechanisms: the Llama models utilize separate weight matrices for query, key, and value projections, whereas Phi-4 employs a single grouped `qkv_proj` layer. We also include **Llama-3-8B-Instruct** both as a slightly larger model and to facilitate comparison with prior work. We specifically use the instruction-tuned or chat versions of these models, as these are the variants subjected to safety alignment procedures which our jailbreak attacks aim to circumvent.  
> We evaluate the susceptibility of these models across four distinct precision formats:
> 1. **FP16** (Baseline): Standard 16-bit floating point unquantized representation.
> 2. **FP8**: 8-bit floating point (E4M3 format, 1 sign, 4 exponent, 3 mantissa bits).
> 3. **INT8**: 8-bit signed integer quantization.
> 4. **INT4**: 4-bit signed integer quantization.  
> We applied symmetric, static, channel-wise quantization to the Linear layers of the models, reflecting common Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) industrial practices.
>
> **ZH**: **5.1 目标模型与量化方案**：为了全面评估量化对故障注入越狱攻击的影响，我们选取了代表不同参数规模与网络架构的主流现代大语言模型。主要测试目标包括 **Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct** 与 **Phi-4-mini-instruct**。这二者兼具先进推理性能与代表性架构差异：Llama 系列在注意力机制中为 Query、Key、Value 投影采用了独立的权重矩阵，而 Phi-4 则采用了合并的分组 `qkv_proj` 投影层。此外，我们还将 **Llama-3-8B-Instruct** 纳入评测体系，以检验稍大规模参数体量下的表现，并方便与历史文献对齐对比。我们统一采用这些模型的指令微调（Instruct/Chat）版本，因为这些版本经过了严格的安全对齐训练，正是越狱攻击要攻破的对象。  
> 我们评估了四种精度格式：
> 1. **FP16（基准格式）**：标准的 16 位半精度浮点未量化状态，代表常规训练与部署基准。
> 2. **FP8**：8 位浮点格式（采用 E4M3 编码规范，包含 1 位符号位、4 位指数位和 3 位尾数位）。
> 3. **INT8**：8 位有符号定点整数格式。
> 4. **INT4**：4 位有符号定点整数格式。  
> 我们对模型的全部线性全连接层（Linear layers）应用了对称、静态、按通道（Channel-wise）的训练后量化（PTQ），这严格遵循了业界生产环境的标准部署规范。

> **EN**: **5.2 Attack Dataset Generation**. To guide the attack optimization towards generating full harmful responses, we constructed a specialized `AttackDataset` containing pairs of harmful queries and target completions, as required by the objective function (Eqn. 2). We sourced the harmful queries from the first 32 samples of the AdvBench dataset [15]. To generate the corresponding target completions, we utilize an uncensored version of WizardLM-30B. For each query, we first took the initial affirmative response prefix provided in AdvBench, appended it to the query, and then prompted WizardLM-30B to continue the generation yielding plausible extended harmful completions.
>
> **ZH**: **5.2 攻击优化数据集构建**：为了引导参数优化生成完整且符合语法逻辑的有害回答，我们构建了包含“有害提示词-目标完整回复”配对的专用 `AttackDataset`，以满足公式 (2) 目标函数的需求。我们从经典基准 AdvBench [15] 中提取了前 32 个具有代表性的恶意查询。为了获取对应的完整目标有害回复，我们利用了未进行内容审查过滤的开源模型 WizardLM-30B-Uncensored：针对每个恶意查询，先拼接 AdvBench 提供的肯定式起手前缀，然后引导 WizardLM-30B 续写生成内容翔实、具有欺骗性且连贯的完整恶意解答。

> **EN**: **5.3 Setup**. For our experiments, both the Bit-Level and Word-Level attacks were executed for $N_{\text{iter}} = 150$ iterations across all models (Llama-3.2-3B, Llama-3-8B, and Phi-4-mini) and their respective precision formats. The Bit-Level attack used $N_{\text{CL}} = 100$ candidate weights per layer with a per-layer evaluation budget of $E_{\text{max}} = 100$. The Word-Level attack tested learning rates (LR) of 50, 100, and 200. The implementation utilized the Hugging Face `Transformers` library [17] with FlashAttention v2. Quantization was performed using Neural Magic’s `llmcompressor` and `compressed-tensors`. All experiments were conducted on NVIDIA A100 80GB GPUs with deterministic flags enabled where possible.
>
> **ZH**: **5.3 实验环境配置**：所有模型及其不同量化版本均执行 $N_{\text{iter}} = 150$ 轮攻击迭代。比特级攻击在每层筛选 $N_{\text{CL}} = 100$ 个候选权重，每层最大评估预算为 $E_{\text{max}} = 100$。词级攻击测试了 50、100 和 200 三组学习率（LR）。代码基于 Hugging Face `Transformers` 与 FlashAttention-2 构建；量化过程采用 Neural Magic 的 `llmcompressor` 与 `compressed-tensors` 库；全部实验在 NVIDIA A100 80GB GPU 算力集群上完成，并尽可能开启确定性随机数种子以确保可复现性。

> **EN**: **5.4 Evaluation Strategy**. To assess attack effectiveness, a standardized evaluation was conducted using an independent dataset and a panel of automated judges:
> - **Evaluation Dataset**: We used the complete **HarmBench** test set [18] for all evaluations, chosen for its rigor and standardized coverage.
> - **Evaluation Metric**: The primary metric is **Attack Success Rate (ASR)**. Harmful queries from HarmBench were input to the attacked model, and full responses were generated.
> - **Automated Judging Panel**: Generated responses were assessed by three state-of-the-art automated judges:
>   1. The official HarmBench classifier (Llama-2 based)
>   2. **Llama Guard 3** (8B) [19]
>   3. **Walled Guard-C** [20]
> Each judge provided a binary harmful/harmless label. ASR was calculated as the fraction of prompts deemed harmful by the respective judge.
>
> **ZH**: **5.4 评估策略**：为了客观评估攻击有效性，我们在独立数据集与多重自动化裁判体系下进行标准化评测：
> - **评测数据集**：采用完整的 **HarmBench** 独立测试集 [18]，确保评测样本与攻击优化所用的 AdvBench 样本完全正交隔离。
> - **评测指标**：以**攻击成功率（Attack Success Rate, ASR）**为核心指标。将 HarmBench 中的恶意查询输入被攻击模型，生成完整的回答。
> - **自动化裁判矩阵**：由三个先进的独立裁判模型给出判决：
>   1. 官方 HarmBench 裁判模型（基于 Llama-2 微调）
>   2. Meta 推出的 **Llama Guard 3**（8B）[19]
>   3. 专注合规判定的 **Walled Guard-C** [20]
> 每个裁判独立输出二值化标签（有害 / 无害）。ASR 定义为被裁判判定为有害的回复占总测试样本的百分比。

#### 6.4 攻击优化数据集的影响 / 6.4 Impact of Attack Optimization Dataset

##### 6.4.1 样本选择敏感性 / 6.4.1 Sensitivity to Sample Selection

> **EN**: To investigate the impact of the specific samples chosen for attack optimization, attacks were conducted using four distinct, non-overlapping 32-sample sets, differentiated by their starting sample index (SSI) in the AdvBench-Completions dataset (SSI=0, 32, 64, and 96). An initial analysis on the less robust FP16 models revealed significant sensitivity. For each of the four sample sets, the peak ASR within 25 iterations was recorded. For Llama-3.2-3B, this ASR range was 8.2% for the Bit-Flip Attack and 17.6% for the Weight Attack. For Phi-4-mini, the ranges were 20.1% and 14.5% respectively. When extended to FP8 and INT8 quantization over 150 iterations (Figure 3), the specific samples used still caused substantial ASR variations, particularly in mid-stage iterations for INT8 and final-stage iterations for FP8.

### 7 结论 / 7 Conclusion

> **EN**: This paper investigated the vulnerability of aligned LMs to jailbreaking via targeted parameter manipulation, comparing bit-level and word-level attacks across FP16, FP8, INT8, and INT4 quantization on Llama-3.2-3B, Phi-4-mini, and Llama-3-8B. Our findings demonstrate that while FP16 models are readily jailbroken (>80% ASR within 25 perturbations), all tested quantization schemes significantly influence attack dynamics and ultimate success. Notably, the highly constrained Bit-Flip Attack proved to be as effective as the Word-Level Attack in achieving jailbreaks on FP16 models. FP8 quantization exhibited the strongest resilience, maintaining ASR below 65% even after 150 bit-flips, while INT8 also offered considerable protection. In contrast, INT4 quantization was significantly less resilient than the 8-bit formats. Analysis also revealed differing architectural attack targets based on quantization schemes. We observed significant variability in ASR between judges, which underlines the need for improved evaluation benchmarks. Furthermore, attack success demonstrated sensitivity to the specific samples chosen for optimization, showing potential for attack improvement. While direct attacks on quantized models proved challenging, jailbreaks induced in FP16 models were often transferable to subsequent 8-bit quantization, though INT4 substantially reduced this transferability. Future work should include physical fault injection and explore broader quantization and defense strategies.
>
> **ZH**: 本文系统探究了安全对齐的语言模型面对定向参数操纵越狱攻击的脆弱性，在 Llama-3.2-3B、Phi-4-mini 和 Llama-3-8B 模型上对比了比特级攻击与词级攻击跨 FP16、FP8、INT8 和 INT4 四种量化精度的攻防动态。我们的研究表明，尽管 FP16 模型极易被攻破（在 25 次微小扰动内 ASR 突破 80%），但所有受试量化方案均对攻击动力学与最终成功率产生了深远影响。值得注意的是，极度严苛的单比特翻转攻击在 FP16 模型上表现出与词级连续权重更新完全相当的强大越狱效能。在所有量化方案中，**FP8 量化展现出最为卓越的物理韧性**，在承受高达 150 次比特翻转后依然将 ASR 压制在 65% 以下；INT8 同样提供了可观的防护屏障。相比之下，INT4 量化的防御韧性明显弱于 8 位格式。结构层面的分析进一步揭示了不同量化方案所驱动的架构攻击靶点转移。此外，我们在不同安全裁判模型之间观察到了显著的判定方差，凸显了构建更精准评测基准的迫切需求。最后，尽管直接在已量化模型上施加攻击具有相当难度，但在 FP16 上诱发的越狱参数在后续转换为 8 位量化时表现出极强的可迁移性，而 INT4 则显著削弱了这种迁移渗透能力。未来工作应将本理论拓展至实际物理芯片行锤击（Rowhammer）验证，并探索更广泛的鲁棒量化与软硬件协同防御策略。

### 与 M3 PI Guard 防护体系的映射与启示

1. **多层协同防御的必要性（Beyond Software Boundaries）**：
   - 现有的提示注入防护（包括大多数基于规则和前缀的护栏）均建立在“模型底层权重绝对可信未被篡改”的基本前提上。
   - 本文证明，攻击者只要通过 Rowhammer 在物理硬件层面诱发少于 25 个比特翻转，模型底层的对齐拒答机制就会被自底向上彻底破坏。因此，M3 PI Guard 的**意图扎根验证器（Intent-Grounded Verifier）**与**提交前动态验证契约（Verify-Before-Commit Contract）**至关重要：即使模型本身因底层参数故障产生越狱偏离，外部独立沙箱与契约门禁仍能阻断未授权操作的物理外溢。
2. **边缘部署与量化选择的安全考量**：
   - 工业界在边缘计算与私有化部署中常盲目推崇低比特量化以压缩开销。本文明确指出，**INT4 量化虽然在推理速度和内存上占优，但在抵御参数故障攻击时极其脆弱，防御表现近乎等同于未对齐模型**。
   - 如果系统面临物理共存或侧信道 Rowhammer 风险，**FP8（E4M3）或加固 INT8** 应作为具备物理级安全韧性的首选量化部署方案。
3. **供应链模型哈希校验（Model Weight Integrity Verification）**：
   - “攻击后量化”的高迁移性揭示了供应链攻击的巨大风险：恶意实体可以在未量化模型中预先注入隐蔽的翻转参数木马，随后量化分发给下游用户。
   - M3 PI Guard 应集成对加载模型权重的密码学哈希签名核验机制，杜绝携带物理越狱参数的受损模型进入生产流水线。
